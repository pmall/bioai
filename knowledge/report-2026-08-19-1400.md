# Report — 2026-08-19

## Findings

- **Anthropic Claude designs protein binders autonomously** (Aug 18, 2026). Claude Mythos Preview and Opus 4.8 designed minibinders against 15 protein targets, succeeding against 14. Wet lab validation by Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience confirmed hit rates of 26.7% (Opus 4.8) and 35.1% (Mythos single-target), vs. 10-15% typical in protein design campaigns. Includes high-affinity binders (KD <10 nM) and binders matching/exceeding best published affinities against at least 4 targets. Claude orchestrated publicly available specialist protein design models end-to-end with minimal human involvement. Also demonstrated species cross-reactive binders against TNFα (challenging therapeutically relevant target) and fold-diverse β-sheet binders. Separately, Claude Opus 5 processed raw NMR and LC-MS files from a contract lab in 23 and 19 minutes, matching the lab's own analysis (96.4% vs 96.33% purity). Anthropic notes life science tasks currently blocked in most capable model, access program coming soon. https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design
