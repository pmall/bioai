## Findings

### Anthropic Claude autonomous protein design: wet-lab validated (Aug 18-20 2026)

Claude Mythos Preview and Opus 4.8 designed minibinders against 15 protein targets. Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience independently validated: 354 binders from 1,320 designs (26.8% overall hit rate). Single-target mode Mythos reached 35.1%. Succeeded on 14 of 15 targets. Against RBX1, Claude's top design bound at 3.9 nM vs. contest winner's 45 nM (~10x tighter). TREM2: 80% hit rate (72/90). TNFα yielded 12 binders (previously zero from other groups). Budget: $50K multi-target, $10K single-target. Claude installed and ran open-source tools (RFdiffusion3, PXDesign, BoltzGen, ProteinMPNN, ESMFold2) autonomously. No human-expert control arm; binding not structurally resolved. Datasets published on Hugging Face. Also demonstrated NMR/LCMS analysis in 23/19 minutes. https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design

### AbCellera ABCL635 Phase 2: best-in-class vasomotor symptom data (Aug 10 2026)

AbCellera's NK3R antagonist antibody ABCL635 met primary endpoints in Phase 2 (92 postmenopausal women). Single 600 mg SC dose: 83% reduction in hot flash frequency vs. 33% placebo (50% placebo-adjusted, p<0.001). Severity reduced 58% vs. 12% placebo (46% adjusted). No serious adverse events, no liver/GI toxicity signals. Stifel analysts: efficacy ~2x that of Astellas Veozah and Bayer Lynkuet (small-molecule NK3R drugs given daily). ABCL635 is an antibody — potential for long-acting quarterly/semi-annual dosing. Plans for Phase 3 in 2027 and studies in treatment-induced VMS (breast/prostate cancer). Stock rose ~31%. First pipeline program from AbCellera's GPCR/ion channel platform. https://investors.abcellera.com/news/news-releases/2026/AbCellera-Announces-Positive-Top-Line-Phase-2-Clinical-Trial-Results-for-ABCL635-Demonstrating-Significant-Reduction-in-Frequency-and-Severity-of-Vasomotor-Symptoms-and-a-Favorable-Tolerability-Profile/default.aspx

### Merck-Moderna mRNA cancer vaccine: first positive Phase 3 for individualized neoantigen therapy (Aug 19 2026)

First positive Phase 3 for an individualized neoantigen therapy and mRNA-based cancer treatment. Builds on Phase 2b data showing 49% reduction in recurrence/death and 59% reduction in distant metastasis/death. Moderna stock surged ~84%. Analysts project multibillion peak sales in melanoma alone. ### Vivodyne: world's largest human tissue data center opens (Aug 12-19 2026)

Vivodyne opened 12 robotic HIVE labs near San Francisco with capacity for 3.1 million controlled human tissue trials per year (claimed 2x all US clinical trials combined). Grows 20+ types of human organ tissue (liver, lungs, gut, bone marrow, pancreas, kidney, eyes, lymph nodes) with autonomous dosing and monitoring. Liver tissue: 94% predictive accuracy for toxicity vs. human trials. Airway tissue: 96% concordance. Bone marrow: 100% concordance on 20 chemo drugs. Raised ~$80M (Khosla Ventures). Eight of the world's largest pharma companies paying for early access. Vision: generate causal biological data to train "world model of human biology" — moving from static snapshots to dynamic, cause-effect training data for AI models. CEO Georgescu: "Absent human testing, these models are going to cure cancer in mice." https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/ai-isnt-close-to-curing-cancer-this-startup-says-it-knows-what-it-will-take/

### SandboxAQ AQPotency: virtual screening without solved protein structures (Aug 19 2026)

AQPotency, a Large Quantitative Model (LQM) for predicting drug potency, launched on Claude via MCP. Runs on ordinary hardware, ranks molecule-target pairs in seconds, costs ~$1 per 1,000 comparisons. Works without solved protein crystal structures — opens programs that structure-based methods cannot reach. Provides confidence intervals with each prediction. Also works in reverse: scans broad protein panels to identify likely targets for a given molecule. Already used in 8 customer programs with experimentally validated impact. Launched alongside AQCat for catalyst discovery. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sandboxaq-launches-aqpotency-bringing-ultrafast-virtual-screening-to-drug-discovery-without-a-solved-protein-structure-302855441.html

### Deep Origin DODock/DOScore: wet-lab validated on novel targets (Aug 19 2026)

Deep Origin published BioRxiv wet-lab validation of its AI+physics virtual screening framework. 31% hit rate against CD73 (cancer immunotherapy enzyme) — ~100x improvement over pure ML approaches. Maintained 50-89% pose accuracy on novel targets where co-folding models drop below 25%. Uses diffusion model for pose proposals + physics-based Vina-style energy function for refinement. Strict double-similarity filter (30% sequence identity, 0.4 Tanimoto) ensures true novelty. Hit compounds showed high scaffold novelty (ECFP4 Tanimoto 0.22-0.27 vs. known binders). Screened against 80B+ compound library. $82M raised. https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/08/19/deep-origin-claims-breakthrough-in-ai-drug-discovery-platform/

### BioMatrix: first natively multimodal foundation model for molecules and proteins (Jun 20 2026)

BioMatrix (arXiv) is the first multimodal foundation model natively integrating sequences, structures, and natural language for both molecules and proteins in a single decoder-only architecture. Built on Qwen3 (1.7B and 4B). Unified tokenization: SMILES/SELFIES for molecules, residue-level + VQ-VAE tokens for proteins, natural language. 304.4B-token pretraining corpus. State-of-the-art on 77/80 tasks across 6 categories (molecular generation/prediction, molecule-text translation, conformer generation, protein understanding/design, structure tasks, interaction prediction). Demonstrates text-based molecule generation, property-conditioned conformer generation, protein sequence-structure co-generation, and structure-grounded affinity prediction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22138v1

### Universal Cell Embedding: cross-species cell biology foundation model (Jul 8 2026)

UCE (Nature) is a 650M-parameter foundation model for single-cell gene expression. Trained self-supervised on 36M cells across 8 species. Maps any cell from any tissue/species into a shared universal space without fine-tuning or annotation. Uses ESM2 protein embeddings to represent genes, enabling zero-shot generalization to unseen species. Created the Integrated Mega-scale Atlas (36M cells, 1,000+ cell types). Exhibits emergent behavior: identifies developmental lineages and embeds data from species not in training. Freely available. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10689-z

### CAPTAIN: multimodal single-cell RNA+protein foundation model (May 7 2026)

CAPTAIN (Nature Comms) is pretrained on 4.2M co-assayed transcriptome+proteome single-cell profiles. Dual-encoder transformer with cross-modal attention. Unified vocabulary of 382 surface proteins. Expands effective proteomic feature space by 4.9x vs. typical CITE-seq panels. Supports protein imputation, cell type annotation, batch harmonization, and protein-informed cell-cell communication inference. Validated on COVID-19 severity analysis. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-72882-y

### MIMIC: generative multimodal foundation model for central dogma (Apr 27 2026)

MIMIC (arXiv, Polymathic AI) uses split-track encoder-decoder architecture to jointly represent nucleic acid, protein, evolutionary, structural, regulatory, and semantic modalities. 1B parameters. Trained on LORE dataset. Supports conditional generation of missing components from observed modalities. Achieves SOTA splicing prediction, isoform-aware inference, and constrained biomolecular design (e.g., corrective edits for HBB splice mutation, PD-L1/hACE2 binding site design). https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2604.24506

### SUCCEED: supervised DNA foundation model (May 14 2026)

SUCCEED (Nature Comms) pretrained on 6,389 ENCODE functional genomics tracks. Hybrid conv-Transformer architecture. Outperforms Enformer on benchmark tasks. Supports cell-type-specific epigenomic prediction, chromatin accessibility denoising, and 3D chromatin contact prediction without CTCF input. Zero-shot cross-species transferability. Demonstrates that supervised pretraining on functional genomics data outperforms self-supervised sequence-only approaches. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73129-6
