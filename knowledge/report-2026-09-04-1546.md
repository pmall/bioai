# Report — 2026-09-04

## Findings

- **2026-09-04** — CluePoints launches three AI-based clinical data review tools (Intelligent Query Detection, Medical and Safety Review, Intelligent Medical Coding) built and validated inside a top 10 pharma partner's live portfolio. IMC targets 99% accuracy on MedDRA suggestions, cuts manual coding effort by ~50%. MSR replaces spreadsheet-driven safety reconciliation with change-based, patient-centric workflow posting queries directly to EDC. Aligns with ICH E6(R3) guidelines. Deploys in days across ongoing studies. Signals AI moving from drug discovery into clinical trial operations layer — the bottleneck is shifting from "find molecules" to "prove they work efficiently." https://www.clinicaltrialvanguard.com/news/cluepoints-launches-ai-tools-for-clinical-data-review-with-top-10-pharma-partner/
