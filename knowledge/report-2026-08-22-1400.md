### Communications Biology review: protein binder design and conformational prediction (May 8 2026)

Review by Abriata (Communications Biology) identifies two emerging frontiers: (1) prediction of full protein conformational landscapes (beyond AlphaFold's static structures) and (2) routine de novo design of high-affinity protein binders. New generative approaches approximate Boltzmann-weighted ensembles at a fraction of molecular dynamics cost. Notes that CASP now includes conformational landscape prediction as a challenge. AI-guided design platforms transforming protein binder development into a "scalable engineering discipline with high experimental success rates." Pre-AI hit rates for computationally designed binders were <0.05%; now routinely characterized in microplates. https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-026-10112-3

### Science review: How AI is reengineering protein engineering (Apr 9 2026)

Listgarten & Jiang review in Science provides unifying statistical framework for understanding AI protein engineering advances. Key insight: the goal is to sample sequences from a property-conditional probability distribution — s ~ p(s | y ∈ Y). Three approaches converge: (1) in silico fitness predictors for guided search, (2) generative models for sequence generation, (3) conditional generative models for property-specific design. Pre-AI hit rates for computationally designed protein binders were <0.05%; now with generative models plus growing PDB, hit rates increased by orders of magnitude. Current challenge: generative models alone not sufficient — field depends heavily on post-hoc filtering by structure predictors and biophysics-based criteria. Generalization to new parts of protein space remains an open question. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aec8444
