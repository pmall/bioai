## Benchmarks and validation

Uses decontaminated data from validated programs, not memorized exam questions. Two suites: Drug Discovery Foundations (300+ evaluations across disease biology, molecular property prediction, retrosynthesis, structure-based design) and Drug Candidate Essentials (end-to-end PCC nomination decisions). Available via standard chat-completions API. Public leaderboard option. https://www.labmate-online.com/news/company-laboratory-news/127/breaking-news/novel-benchmark-launched-to-test-whether-ai-models-can-truly-discover-drugs/68214

- **Nature Reviews Drug Discovery perspective** (Aug 7) — Bender, Thomas, Scannell et al. publish critical review: evidence of clinically relevant impact from AI is "disappointingly limited." Argues benchmarks must move from model validation to measuring decision-making improvement. Identifies insufficient focus on clinical translation, conditional life science data challenges, and "technology push" vs "science pull" as core problems. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41573-026-01496-2

## Protein design and engineering

- **ProteinDPO** (Aug 14, Nature Methods) — Direct preference optimization aligns a protein language model (ESM-IF1) with experimental stability data. Trained on 660K variants from Megascale dataset, generalizes to large multichain complexes and antibodies. Applied to H5N1 influenza HA: 80% of designs improved stability, up to 32°C improvement in Tm from recent mammalian strains. Open-source. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03137-3

- **Raygun** (Jul 29, Nature) — Generative framework for miniaturizing, modifying, and augmenting proteins. Encodes proteins as probability distributions in fixed dimensions (length-agnostic). Shrinks fluorescent proteins by 10-25%+ while preserving function (6/8 variants fluoresced). Miniaturized TurboID retained ligase activity. Enlarged EGF achieved higher binding affinity than wild type. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10842-8

- **AI-redesigned starting points enhance evolution** (Jul 22, Nature) — ProteinMPNN redesign of BoNT proteases creates more mutationally robust starting points for directed evolution. Redesigned enzymes consistently outperformed wild-type in phage-assisted continuous evolution. Best ataxin-2-cleaving protease evolved from redesigned starting point achieved 79-fold greater selected specificity. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10820-0

- **Antibody-specific language model** (Aug 13) — BU researchers build 600M-parameter model focused on CDR regions using 1.6M paired antibody sequences. Preferential CDR masking strategy improved binding affinity prediction by 27% across 90K+ engineered variants. Far less compute than larger antibody models. https://phys.org/news/2026-08-ai-biology-antibodies-drug-discovery.html

- **Synthetic gene editors** (Aug 11, Nature Biotechnology) — ESM-IF1 inverse folding combined with evolutionary coupling analysis generates non-natural TnpB variants (SynTnpBs) with 50-60% sequence identity to natural. Higher editing activity than natural TnpBs in bacterial, plant, and human cells. Reveals new DNA-bound conformational state. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03273-3

## Single-cell and spatial biology

- **VirTues** (Aug 5, Nature) — Foundation model for spatial proteomics. Marker-aware representations from 15 IMC cohorts (3,102 patients, 146 markers), extends across 4 imaging technologies. Zero-shot cell typing, niche annotation, marker reconstruction. TNBC biomarkers predict anti-PD-L1 response (AUROC 0.823), outperform current clinical stratification. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10884-y

- **scDMC** (Aug 8, Genome Biology) — Dual-stream single-cell foundation model. Processes rank-based and expression-aware sequences in parallel. Pre-trained on only 2M cells, sets SOTA in cell annotation, clustering, integration. Uncovers functional gene modules and cell-type-specific regulatory networks. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13059-026-04193-w

## Clinical translation

- **Rentosertib Phase III** — Insilico's AI-discovered TNIK inhibitor for IPF continues enrolling 320 patients across 47 China centers. Phase IIa (Nature Medicine 2025) showed +98.4 mL FVC improvement at 12 weeks. 15-20 AI-derived programs expected to enter pivotal trials in 2026. No AI-discovered drug has received FDA approval yet. https://artificialscience.org/2026/08/first-ai-designed-drug-phase-3-rentosertib/

## Key observation

The field is bifurcating into two tracks: (1) benchmarking and validation efforts (Insilico DDD BaaS, Nature Reviews perspective) that demand AI prove clinical decision-making value, not just model performance; and (2) rapid capability expansion in protein design (ProteinDPO, Raygun), virtual screening (Deep Origin), and agentic workflows (Pathos, Phylo-Chugai, Insilico VAC). The gap between "impressive demos" and "approved medicines" remains the central tension, with rentosertib Phase III as the defining test case.
