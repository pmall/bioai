## Findings

### Sanofi SWEL: enterprise lab-in-the-loop on AWS (Aug 19-20)

Sanofi and AWS unveiled Scientific Workflow Experience Labs (SWEL), a context-aware scientific operating system that connects data, workflows, models, and AI agents across the drug discovery lifecycle. SWEL is built on Sanofi's Scientific Information Processing Services (SIPS), which manages 20+ petabytes of scientific data. The platform provides shared memory and continuity across the discovery lifecycle through SWEL Copilot, an R&D agentic studio built on Bedrock AgentCore. SWEL aspires to reduce molecules requiring wet lab validation by 50%, increase AI workflow deployment speed by 10x, and enable 2-3x more in silico projects. The platform has already contributed to identifying biologically active compounds in discovery projects and has scaled to 50+ scientific workflows. Aspires to compress discovery cycles from years to weeks.
- https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/industries/enterprise-lab-in-the-loop-on-aws-how-sanofi-is-compressing-drug-discovery-from-years-to-weeks/

### Aizen Therapeutics oral peptide deal (Aug 15)

Aizen Therapeutics entered a multi-program collaboration with an unnamed San Diego-based public biotech to design oral peptide therapeutics using its DaX foundation model. Several million dollars in initial revenue plus up to $100M in milestones per nominated target. DaX is a generative AI foundation model trained on millions of annotated molecules and receptors, exploring non-canonical amino acid (ncAA) peptide chemical space at 10x the scale of traditional ncAA discovery methods. Aizen is a Caltech spinout focused on oral biologics for immunology and neurology. The use of ncAAs confers metabolic stability and membrane permeability for oral bioavailability.
- https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aizen-enters-into-collaboration-with-san-diego-biopharma-to-advance-oral-biologics-302848775.html

### Beyang Therapeutics $30M Series A (Aug 12)

Beyang Therapeutics closed an oversubscribed $30M Series A financing, co-led by Legend Capital and Shanghai Healthcare Capital. Beyang's ExCEED platform integrates generative AI, computational simulation, and ADMET prediction into a closed-loop molecular design and optimization engine. The platform can generate entirely novel scaffolds, not just iterate on existing cores. Pipeline: BT01001 (small-molecule eye drop for retinal disease, Phase I complete, IND cleared by US FDA and China NMPA) and BT01002 (second-generation Menin inhibitor for KMT2A-rearranged/NPM1-mutated acute leukemia, overcoming acquired resistance mutations, IND-enabling studies). Ranked 8th globally among 350+ participants in OpenADMET blind prediction challenge.
- https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/beyang-closes-oversubscribed-30-million-series-a-financing

Pathos also entered a collaboration with AstraZeneca on AZD4241 (ERα PROTAC). Foundry uses Pathos's Oncology Foundation Model trained on 200+ PB of multimodal data with Tempus's 8M+ patient records.

### AI drug patent inventorship: unresolved legal vulnerability (Aug 21)

MIT Technology Review investigation documents the gap between AI drug companies' public claims and patent filings. Insilico Medicine claims rentosertib was "discovered by" its AI platform but names five humans as patent inventors. US courts (DABUS, 2022) ruled only humans can be inventors. The USPTO now treats AI as a tool — no disclosure required. Ryan Abbott (DABUS attorney) argues incorrect inventor listing could invalidate patents. If AI-generated drug structures can't be adequately protected, it undermines the commercial rationale for AI drug discovery investment. Companies are keeping humans in the loop and documenting carefully, but as AI autonomy increases, the legal framework will be tested.
- https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/08/21/1142627/when-ai-designs-a-drug-who-gets-the-credit/
