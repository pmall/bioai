## Findings

- **2026-09-08** — BioSpace opinion (Thomas M. Kaiser, Avicenna Biosciences): Foundation models will change drug discovery only under certain conditions, not as claimed by AI leaders. Two roadblocks: irreproducible literature corroding training data (Nature, MD Anderson analyses), and hidden cause-effect shifts (Bertrand's / Simpson's paradoxes, "magic methyl" — same chemical tweak helps one series, hurts another, shown in Avicenna arXiv 2602.23303). Cites BCG study: 1 foundation model in Q4 2021 → 226 in Q2 2025 in chemistry/biology/medicine. Rejects Anthropic Claude Science "100 years in 10 years" framing — would require 470+ drug approvals/year for 10 years. Where models can bear fruit: novel biological network target ID and mining clinical data for patient selection (Yatiri Bio, Bruker + Noetik tissue foundation models), with experimental rigor remaining with scientists. Signals field-defining self-critique continuing after Bender et al. Nature Reviews Drug Discovery perspective. https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/opinion-ai-will-only-revolutionize-drug-discovery-and-development-if-we-play-to-its-strengths

- **2026-09-08** — Moonwalk Biosciences $70M Series B for adipose-targeted RNAi obesity (MW101): Led by Alpha Wave + YK Bioventures, with Eli Lilly, Gaorong, ARCH, Khosla, Future Ventures. Lead MW101 uses siRNA targeting adipose tissue (not appetite suppression); preclinical mice showed weight/fat loss while preserving muscle without reducing food intake. First-in-human planned late 2027. Discovery engine is AI platform combining genetics, epigenomics and omics to find siRNA reducing adipose fat without GLP-1 gastric/muscle-loss effects. Founded 2024 with $57M seed backed by Feng Zhang. Signals non-incretin "quality weight loss" wave (muscle preservation, durable infrequent dosing) plus AI-designed siRNA entering obesity after Alnylam-Inceptive $2B deal. https://www.biospace.com/business/moonwalk-pulls-in-70m-to-take-fat-targeting-rnai-obesity-candidate-to-clinic
