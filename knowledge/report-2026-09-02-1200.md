## Findings

- **2026-09-02** — Helmholtz Munich and Technical University of Munich publish in Nature: Synthetic Transfer Vehicles (STVs) — an entirely new class of RNA transporters built from the ground up using AI-designed proteins. The team combined natural protein building blocks with synthetic protein structures designed using generative AI that form non-natural geometries. STV-C8 was the most efficient variant, outperforming tested virus-like particles and lipid nanoparticles in cell culture RNA delivery. In mice, IV delivery produced mainly lung expression without detected toxicity or immune effects. In pig muscle tissue, CRISPR/Cas9 cargo delivered via STV removed a dystrophin gene. Plans to transfer technology into a spin-off company. Signals AI-designed proteins are now enabling new drug delivery modalities — not just therapeutic proteins, but the delivery vehicles themselves, with non-natural geometries outperforming nature-evolved structures. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10952-3

- **2026-09-01** — BiteNetI (Constructor University, Communications Biology): physics-based AI model that maps ion binding sites across proteins using image-recognition technology adapted from smartphone cameras. Trained on 12,000 protein complexes with 35,000+ precisely mapped ions. Scans protein 3D structure across 11 atom types. Available free as web app at bitenet-ion.imolecule.app. Every binding site is a potential drug intervention point; current tools predict them class-by-class (small molecules, peptides, ions) each with separate models. Next step: one general model recognizing all binding site types. Signals AI expanding from structure prediction to functional annotation — identifying how ions control protein function, which matters for drug design targeting ion-dependent proteins and understanding mutation effects. https://dx.doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10659-1

- **2026-09-01** — New method for enhancing AlphaFold3 conformational sampling published (Institute for Molecular Science). Introduces a bias strategy into AF3's diffusion generative model to capture previously missed structural states — the shapes conventional predictions often miss. Addresses one of AlphaFold3's key limitations: its bias toward static snapshots rather than the conformational ensemble proteins adopt in reality. Signals the field is moving beyond single-structure prediction toward dynamics-aware structural biology, complementing experiment-guided approaches from Nature Biotechnology and Nature Methods earlier in 2026.

- **2026-09-02** — BenchLM Protein Design benchmark updated: Claude Opus 5 still leads at 42.5%. Only 1 model evaluated so far on this constrained protein-sequence design benchmark (constraint satisfaction, folding confidence, sequence novelty without tool access). Quarterly refresh cadence. Signals the benchmark is still in early adoption phase — the absence of competing models means it's not yet a meaningful differentiator, but the framework is in place for when more models are evaluated.

The timeline to primary completion (Oct 2029) remains aggressive if enrollment is delayed.
