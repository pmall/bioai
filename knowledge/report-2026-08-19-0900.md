Signals Novo is building capabilities across the full computing spectrum — simulation (NVIDIA), broad model access (OpenAI), and secure cloud infrastructure (AWS). https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/novo-nordisk-and-aws-sign-ai-drug-discovery-pact-launch-london-innovation-hub

- **DaltonTx launches Dalton platform** (Aug 18, 2026). UK startup emerged from stealth, founded by former AstraZeneca and Exscientia scientists (CEO Garry Pairaudeau, 20 years AZ R&D, former CTO at Exscientia). Dalton handles full drug discovery process from raw data to molecular design and decision-making. Key differentiator: data privacy — project data/models kept separate, not used to train other models. First clients: Sygnature Discovery (CRO) and Bonito Biosciences (CNS therapies). Positions as enablement platform rather than pipeline developer. https://pharmaphorum.com/news/meet-dalton-new-ai-powered-drug-discovery-platform

- **Protein design biosecurity concerns** (Frontiers in Microbiology, Apr 2026). Review warns AI-generated proteins may be functionally equivalent to known toxins while sharing little sequence similarity, rendering current homology-based screening blind. Current DNA synthesis screening relies on sequence similarity — AI-designed proteins can bypass this. Calls for layered mitigation: structure-based screening, built-in model safeguards, watermarking, and functional assays. Notes open-source availability of design tools lowers barrier to misuse. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2026.1817535/full

- **Proximity therapeutics emerging as AI frontier** (2026). Proxima (formerly VantAI, Roivant spinout) raised $80M seed in Jan 2026 (DCVC, NVentures/NVIDIA, Roivant). Platform combines cross-linking mass spectrometry (NeoLink) with AI model Neo-1 for designing molecular glues and PROTACs. Multi-billion dollar collaborations with J&J, BMS, and Blueprint Medicines. First partnered program expected in clinic in 2026. Building agentic workflows using NVIDIA BioNeMo Agent Toolkit. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260113074220/en/Proxima-Raises-%2480-Million-Led-by-DCVC-to-Power-the-Next-Generation-of-Proximity-Based-Medicines

First fully AI-designed drug posted positive Phase IIa efficacy data (rentosertib). 25 companies actually delivering clinical candidates. However, only 6.8% of AI assets completed Phase 2. https://biomednexus.com/ai-drug-discovery-companies-clinical-candidates-2026/

- **Sequence Display enables 10M+ protein data points in 3 days** (Nature Biotechnology, Apr 2026). High-throughput method from Rice University generates massive sequence-activity datasets for training protein engineering AI models. Overcomes key bottleneck: lack of experimental training data. Successfully demonstrated with multiple proteins including aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases. https://phys.org/news/2026-04-protein-breakthrough-generates-10m-turbocharges.html
