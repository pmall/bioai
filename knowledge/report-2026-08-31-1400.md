Picomolar leads across Fabs, scFvs, mAbs, bispecifics, and ADC-enabling binders. Anti-VEGF scFv-IgG outperformed bevacizumab reference. Signals sequence-only protein design reaching production-scale validation with high throughput (100+ concurrent programs). https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/ainnocence-reports-91-4-program-success-rate-across-58-antibody-affinity-maturation-campaigns-opens-28-program-biologics-pipeline-to-licensing
