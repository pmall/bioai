- **2026-09-07** — NEAT-POCKET (arXiv): Pocket-conditioned autoregressive 3D molecular generation using Neighborhood-Guided Efficient Autoregressive Set Transformer. Generates molecules atom by atom within protein binding pockets while maintaining atom permutation invariance and explicitly modeling hydrogen atoms. Competitive performance on CrossDocked and SPINDR benchmarks with significantly faster sampling than existing methods. Supports pocket-conditioned fragment completion for lead optimization. Signals autoregressive set transformer approaches emerging for structure-based molecular generation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05097

- **2026-09-03** — SimpleDesign (TMLR 2026): Joint protein sequence and structure co-design model trained directly in data space (no multi-stage latent training). Mixture-of-Transformer architecture with modality-specific processing plus global self-attention. Single-stage end-to-end objective combining discrete cross-entropy (sequences) and regression (structures). Trained on 2M+ sequence-structure pairs. Strong performance on co-design and unconditional generation benchmarks. Signals that simpler, single-stage training paradigms may match multi-stage approaches for protein co-design. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03377

- **2026-09-01** — SymFold (arXiv): Symmetric dual-path architecture for protein inverse folding that synergizes evolutionary (PLM) and structural (MPLM) priors through iterative refinement. Addresses limitation that MPLMs alone are not effective for inverse folding. Joint training outperforms serial pipeline approaches. Signals that combining pretrained sequence and structural knowledge through iterative refinement improves inverse folding accuracy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01353

- **2026-09-01** — PROSOUNDS (ECCB 2026): Uncertainty-weighted activation steering of protein language models for robust protein design. Uses probabilistic surrogate model with epistemic uncertainty quantification to reweight steering vector contributions — confident predictions count more, unreliable guesses count less. Projects out domain-specific bias for better OOD performance. Tested on toxicity reduction, solubility enhancement, and immunogenicity improvement — consistently beats SOTA baselines. Signals that uncertainty-aware methods can make protein language model steering more reliable in real-world settings with scarce/noisy labels. https://biomlsp.com/2026/09/01/uncertainty-weighted-activation-steering-of-protein-language-models-enables-robust-protein-design/

- **2026-08-28** — DyAb (MAbs journal): Sequence-based antibody design and property prediction in low-data regimes using pair-wise representation. Takes pairs of protein sequences, computes relative embeddings, predicts affinity differences rather than absolute values. Spearman ρ up to 0.85 on binding affinity prediction with as few as 100 training examples. Generates antibody variants that express and bind at >85% rates, with many improving on parent affinity by >10-fold. Demonstrated on EGFR, IL-6, and internal targets. Signals that relative/pairwise representation learning can make antibody optimization effective in data-scarce early-stage drug development. https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19420862.2026.2717460
