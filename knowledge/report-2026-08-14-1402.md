### Isomorphic Labs — IsoDDE and Series B

**Source:** https://isomorphiclabs.com/articles/the-isomorphic-labs-drug-design-engine-unlocks-a-new-frontier

Isomorphic Labs (Alphabet/DeepMind spinoff, founded by Demis Hassabis) published details of the Isomorphic Labs Drug Design Engine (IsoDDE) on February 10, 2026. IsoDDE is a unified computational drug-design system that goes beyond AlphaFold 3. Key reported capabilities:

- **Structure prediction generalization:** More than doubles AlphaFold 3's accuracy on the "Runs N' Poses" benchmark for protein-ligand structures dissimilar to training data (the hardest 0-20% similarity bin). Successfully predicts induced fits and cryptic pocket openings — including recapitulating a recently discovered cryptic allosteric pocket on cereblon (Dippon et al., 2026, Nature) from sequence alone.
- **Antibody-antigen prediction:** Outperforms AlphaFold 3 by 2.3x and Boltz-2 by 19.8x at high-fidelity DockQ > 0.8 on a low-homology antibody-antigen test set. Shows particular strength on the CDR-H3 loop.
- **Binding affinity prediction:** Surpasses all deep-learning methods on three public benchmarks (FEP+ 4, OpenFE, CASP16 blind binding affinity). Matches or exceeds physics-based FEP methods without requiring experimental crystal structures as input.
- **Blind pocket identification:** Identifies novel ligandable pockets using only amino acid sequence, approaching experimental fragment-soaking performance in seconds.

IsoDDE technical report: https://storage.googleapis.com/isomorphiclabs-website-public-artifacts/isodde_technical_report.pdf

**Source:** https://isomorphiclabs.com/articles/isomorphic-labs-announces-series-b-investment-round

### Recursion Pharmaceuticals — Platform and Pipeline

**Source:** https://www.recursion.com/

Recursion (Salt Lake City) operates what it calls the Recursion Operating System — a vertical AI drug discovery platform built on massive proprietary data. Key facts:

- **Data scale:** 65+ petabytes from 300+ million experiments. Automated wet labs run ~2.2 million experiments per week across 50 human cell types. Also streams video from animal behavioral studies.
- **Compute:** BioHive-2, built with NVIDIA, is the most powerful supercomputer in biopharma.
- **Pipeline:** 7+ clinical-stage programs including:
  - REC-3565 (MALT1 inhibitor) — Phase 1, first patient dosed for relapsed/refractory B-cell lymphomas
  - FAP (TUPELO trial) — Familial adenomatous polyposis
  - Solid tumors (ELUCIDATE, DAHLIA, ENLYGHT trials)
  - B-cell malignancies (EXCELERIZE trial)
- **Partnerships:** Genentech (neuroscience target unlocked), plus various pharma and tech partnerships.
- **Acquisitions:** Absorbed Exscientia (merged Sep 2024) and multiple other companies to broaden capabilities.

**Source:** https://fortune.com/2025/04/03/recursion-pharmaceuticals-ai-drug-discovery/

Fortune profile (April 2025) provides broader context on the AI drug discovery race:
- Over $30.6 billion invested across 446 financing rounds in AI-driven life sciences since 2020.
- At least 75 drugs/vaccines from AI-first biotechs entered clinical trials as of early 2024.
- BCG analysis: AI-native companies show 80-90% Phase 1 success rates vs. 40-65% industry average; Phase 2 rates comparable.
- Recursion's REC-994 (CCM) Phase 2 results were mixed — safe/tolerable but ambiguous efficacy signals. Stock dropped 17%, then partially recovered on follow-up data showing functional improvement.
- No AI-discovered drug has reached market yet. The field is still in early-to-mid clinical proof stage.
- Other notable entrants: Insitro ($2.4B valuation, Daphne Koller), Xaira ($1B launch, April 2024), Manas AI (Reid Hoffman + Siddhartha Mukherjee).
- Jack Scannell's "Eroom's Law" remains the backdrop: pharma R&D productivity has halved roughly every 9 years for seven decades.

### Insilico Medicine — Pipeline and Platform

**Source:** https://www.insilico.com/

Insilico Medicine (Hong Kong/Boston/Abu Dhabi/Shanghai) continues to operate its Pharma.ai suite (PandaOmics for target discovery, Chemistry42 for molecular generation, inClinico for clinical trial prediction). Their pipeline includes 25+ programs:

- **TNIK inhibitor** — Fibrotic diseases of lung and kidney (lead program)
- **USP1 inhibitor** — BRCA-mutant cancer
- **KAT6 inhibitor** — ER+/HER2- breast cancer
- **PHD inhibitors** — IBD and chronic kidney disease
- **MAT2A inhibitor** — MTAP-deficient cancer
- **TEAD inhibitor** — Mesothelioma and solid tumors
- Multiple earlier-stage programs across oncology and inflammation

Their IPF program (ISM001-055) was previously reported as the first wholly AI-discovered and AI-designed drug to reach Phase 2, with Phase 2a results showing safety and dose-dependent lung function improvement over 12 weeks (not statistically significant). U.S. Phase 2 ongoing.

Also notable: Insilico launched "Science AI Bench" and "Drug Discovery Benchmark" platforms, and operates PreciousGPT (a Large Language of Life Model).

## Notes for buffers

The field's center of gravity this month is structural prediction advancing into actual drug design. IsoDDE's leap over AlphaFold 3 on generalization benchmarks is the technical headline — the gap between "predicting known structures" and "navigating novel biological space" is exactly what drug discovery requires. The $2.1B Series B signals that investors believe this gap is closeable.

The clinical pipeline across AI-native companies remains early. Recursion has 7+ programs in trials but no readouts that decisively prove the platform. Insilico's IPF data is promising but preliminary. The Fortune piece's Eroom's Law framing is the honest backdrop: no amount of AI changes the fundamental timeline of human clinical trials.

The industry structure is clarifying: Isomorphic Labs owns the structural biology frontier (AlphaFold lineage, pharma partnerships with Novartis/Lilly/J&J). Recursion owns the phenomics-to-pipeline vertical (massive data + compute + wet lab). Insilico Medicine owns the full-stack AI-first pipeline (target → molecule → trial prediction). Boltz-2 (MIT, open-source) represents the academic/open alternative.
