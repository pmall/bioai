### AI expanding from drug discovery into bioprocessing: Cytiva CTO perspective (Aug 25)

Beate Mueller-Tiemann, CTO at Cytiva, published a forward-looking piece arguing generative AI's impact is extending from drug discovery into bioprocessing and manufacturing. Key signals: (1) AI models predicting manufacturability — how easily a protein can be expressed, purified, and formulated — reducing downstream risks; (2) Tahoe Therapeutics raised $30M to build AI models of cells, targeting DeepMind's ambition for a virtual cell within five years; (3) federated learning proposed as the solution for the "low n" problem in bioprocessing, where each manufacturing run generates vast data but the number of runs is small; (4) the vision of fully in-silico process development and digital twins for biomanufacturing is "closer than many realise." The piece frames AI not as a tool but as a strategic enabler for the next generation of biopharmaceutical innovation, requiring a foundational shift toward predictive and scalable manufacturing.

### Systematic benchmarking of 12 AI molecular generation methods (bioRxiv, Aug 17)

A comprehensive benchmarking study evaluated 12 molecular generation and optimization methods across 176 curated protein-ligand systems spanning diverse therapeutic target classes. Methods included pocket-conditioned 3D generation, diffusion and flow-based modeling, autoregressive construction, reference-conditioned optimization, and synthesis-aware design. Performance assessed using operational robustness, chemical validity, uniqueness, molecular and scaffold diversity, QED, synthetic accessibility, docking, physicochemical and ADMET properties, and computational resource requirements. Provides practical guidelines for generative AI-based preclinical drug development. Important because it moves beyond headline benchmarks to operational metrics that matter for actual drug discovery workflows.
- https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.14.744939v1
