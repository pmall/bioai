## Synthetic biology & genomics

- **AI-designed bacteriophage genomes** (Drug Target Review, Aug 10; Science) — Stanford/Arc Institute (Brian Hie lab) used Evo genomic language models to design complete functional bacteriophage genomes from scratch. 16 functional phages identified from hundreds designed. Engineered phages overcame bacterial resistance to naturally occurring ΦX174-like phages in two E. coli strains. Demonstrates AI can engineer entire biological systems, not just individual genes. Biosafety and biosecurity implications significant. https://www.drugtargetreview.com/ai-designs-functional-bacteriophage-genomes-to-overcome-bacterial-resistance/2136186.article

## Key observation

The field is bifurcating on commercial strategy. Insilico's "drug dealer" model — high-volume preclinical candidate generation for licensing at the preclinical stage — represents one path. The tech-bio companies (Isomorphic, Generate, NewLimit) pursue the full pipeline. Both models are attracting capital, but the next 12 months will determine which produces clinical proof. Meanwhile, AI is expanding beyond molecules to entire genomes — the bacteriophage genome design work signals a new frontier where AI designs not just proteins or drugs, but complete biological systems.
