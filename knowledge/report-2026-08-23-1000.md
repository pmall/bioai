### MIT Technology Review: When AI designs a drug, who gets the credit? (Aug 21 2026)

Deep analysis of the patent inventorship problem for AI-designed drugs. Insilico Medicine claimed rentosertib was "discovered by" its generative AI platform in press releases, but named five humans (including CEO Zhavoronkov) as inventors on the patent. US courts ruled (DABUS case, 2022) that only "individuals" can be inventors under US statute. The USPTO under Trump administration now treats AI as merely a tool (like a calculator) — no need to even mention it. Ryan Abbott (who brought the DABUS case) warns that incorrect inventor listing could be grounds for patent invalidation. Isomorphic Labs' chief business officer Sarah Korman says "there is no doubt our laws will need to evolve." The article frames a tension: if AI-generated outputs can't be protected, it could dampen drug development investment. Currently companies are keeping humans in the loop and documenting carefully. https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/08/21/1142627/when-ai-designs-a-drug-who-gets-the-credit/

### Enveda ENV-308: First pill designed from the chemistry of exercise — Phase 1 positive (Aug 18 2026)

ENV-308 is the first drug in human trials designed to mimic Lac-Phe, a pseudo dipeptide hormone released by the body during intense exercise. Phase 1 enrolled 88 healthy volunteers: exceptional safety across full dose range, no serious AEs, no discontinuations. Notably excellent GI safety profile — critical because GI side effects are the main reason people stop GLP-1 drugs. Exploratory biomarker: reduced circulating leptin (signal of metabolic relevance). Animal data: preserved lean muscle during weight loss, prevented weight regain after stopping therapy. Enveda plans Phase 2 to test weight maintenance after GLP-1 discontinuation, plus exploratory studies in migraine, IBD, and PCOS. Discovered using Enveda's AI-enabled platform. https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/enveda-reports-positive-phase-1-results-for-env-308-the-first-pill-designed-from-the-chemistry-of-exercise

### Sravathi AI + Mayo Clinic: AI targets "undruggable" GIPC1 protein (Aug 20 2026)

Bengaluru-based Sravathi AI and Mayo Clinic developed an experimental drug targeting the PDZ-domain of GIPC1 protein, which contributes to pancreatic and other cancers. AI platform screened ~40,000 compounds using generative AI, predictive modelling, and physics-based chemistry. In lab and animal models: slowed tumor progression, improved survival, enhanced gemcitabine effect, and showed preliminary signs of altering tumor microenvironment. Senior author Debabrata Mukhopadhyay at Mayo Clinic: "AI-assisted discovery can help identify treatment options against targets that have historically been considered undruggable." Sravathi has 9 experimental candidates across pancreatic, kidney, TNBC, and solid tumors. Further safety testing needed before human trials. https://pharma.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/pharma-industry/sravathi-ai-mayo-clinic-develop-new-molecule-against-undruggable-cancer-target/133362018

### Bioxytran PLICID: First CIDP patient shows functional improvement (Aug 11 2026)

First patient treated with PLICID, an AI-designed galectin antagonist for CIDP (Chronic Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy). The patient regained use of fingers and ability to walk several miles. No adverse events. PLICID is a complex carbohydrate drug designed using Bioxytran's "Artificial Super Intelligence" platform developed with University of Minnesota — interprets NMR data to construct novel carbohydrate structures targeting specific galectins. Single-patient observation; larger study planned. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1445815/000149315226036977/ex99-1.htm

### Nuritas PeptiSleep: First AI-discovered sleep ingredient peer-reviewed (Aug 11 2026)

Peer-reviewed pilot study published in Sleep Medicine: X on PeptiSleep™, a rice protein hydrolysate containing AI-discovered bioactive peptides from brown rice. 26 adults, 8-week RCT. Novel element: participants wore Oura Rings tracking 15+ biometrics passively at home. Results: improvements in LSEQ sleep initiation (p=0.050) and maintenance (p=0.036) domains. Wearable data showed trends toward higher HRV, lower nocturnal heart rate, and more deep sleep. Total mood disturbance reduced (p=0.029). First in silico-predicted plant peptide ingredient tested in placebo-controlled trial with wearable validation. Nuritas partners include GNC, Mars, Nestlé, L'Oréal. https://www.nuritas.com/nuritas-published-peer-reviewed-peptisleep-sleep-study-in-sleep-medicine-x/

### Artera DIRECT-AI: First patient enrolled in AI prostate cancer registry (Aug 14 2026)

First nationwide registry evaluating clinical utility of an AI-powered cancer test. ArteraAI Prostate Test uses multimodal AI on digitized histopathology + clinical data to provide both prognostic and predictive insights. Non-interventional: patients receive test as standard care, no additional procedures. Data collected across US sites on how AI-powered information influences treatment decisions, shared decision-making, and long-term outcomes. First patient enrolled at The Urology Place in San Antonio, TX. First test of whether AI diagnostic tools change real-world clinical behavior, not just whether they're accurate. https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/artera-enrolls-first-patient-in-direct-ai-registry-marking-milestone-in-real-world-evidence-generation-for-ai-powered-prostate-cancer-care
