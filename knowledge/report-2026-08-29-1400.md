## Findings

- **2026-08-28** — Genentech licenses DualityBio's DUPAC novel-payload ADC platform for $45M upfront, >$1B milestones plus tiered royalties. DUPAC generates payloads with novel mechanisms of action designed to work in tumors resistant to topoisomerase inhibitor-based ADCs (the payload class underlying Enhertu, Trodelvy, etc.). As ADCs move into earlier lines of therapy, a growing pool of patients progress on existing treatments and become less responsive. DualityBio handles discovery and early global development through Phase Ia; Genentech takes over for further development and commercialization. This is DualityBio's seventh major pharma partnership (previously BioNTech, BeiGene, GSK). DualityBio went public on HKEX in 2025 and has 3,500+ patients enrolled across ~20 countries. Signals the ADC resistance problem is now being addressed as a distinct therapeutic category, and Chinese biotech platforms are supplying the novel payload innovation. https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/genentech-doubles-down-adc-resistant-cancers-1b-dualitybio-pact

- **2026-08-24** — Roche licenses Hanmi Pharm's HM17321, a non-incretin UCN2 analog, for $190M upfront, up to $2.3B in milestones plus tiered royalties. HM17321 activates CRF2 receptors in skeletal muscle to simultaneously reduce fat mass and preserve lean body mass — directly addressing the lean mass loss side effect of GLP-1 incretin therapies (Wegovy, Zepbound). Phase 1 enrolling 90 healthy volunteers. Hanmi retains South Korea rights. Roche now has three obesity strategies: Carmot (acquired), Zealand petrelintide (licensed), and now Hanmi HM17321. Signals the obesity market is pivoting from "maximum weight loss" to "quality weight loss" — body composition matters, not just the scale. Non-incretin mechanisms are the next battleground. https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/roche-pens-25b-pact-hanmis-clinical-stage-obesity-drug-targeting-less-used-mechanism

- **2026-08-28** — FDA approves Eli Lilly's Mounjaro (tirzepatide) to reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (CV death, heart attack, stroke) in adults with type 2 diabetes at high CV risk. First and only dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist proven to lower MACE risk. Based on SURPASS-CVOT trial (13,299 patients, ~5 years). Matches Novo's 2020 Ozempic CV approval on a similar timeline. ~40M Americans have T2D; ~1/3 have undetected cardiovascular disease. Expands Mounjaro's addressable market significantly. Signals GLP-1/GIP class is now validated for cardiovascular benefit, not just diabetes and obesity. https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fda-approves-lillys-mounjaro-tirzepatide-reduce-cardiovascular

- **2026-08-27** — FDA approves Priovant's LISRAYA (brepocitinib) 30mg for adults with dermatomyositis. First targeted therapy specifically indicated for this disease, following decades of failed Phase 3 attempts with rituximab, abatacept, tocilizumab, and ustekinumab in myositis populations. Brepocitinib is a dual TYK2/JAK1 inhibitor — suppresses type I/II interferon, IL-6, IL-12, IL-23 signaling. Once-daily oral pill. VALOR Phase 3 trial showed significant improvement in muscle and skin disease activity, physical function, and corticosteroid tapering vs. placebo over 52 weeks. Available immediately in the U.S. Also in Phase 3 for cutaneous sarcoidosis (BEACON+) and Phase 2b/3 for lichen planopilaris. Signals that targeted small-molecule immunomodulation is now reaching rare autoimmune diseases previously considered intractable. https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/27/3352364/0/en/priovant-announces-fda-approval-of-lisraya-brepocitinib-for-adults-with-dermatomyositis-now-available-in-the-u-s.html
