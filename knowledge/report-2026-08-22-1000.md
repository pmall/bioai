### Northwestern DREAM Cloud Lab: NSF awards $20M for AI protein engineering hub (Jul 23 2026)

NSF awarded Northwestern University $20 million to establish the DREAM (AI-Driven, Rapid, Experimental Automation Machine) Cloud Lab — the nation's first publicly accessible, AI-powered cloud laboratory for protein engineering. Part of a $380M national Programmable Cloud Laboratory network (20 inaugural test beds). The facility will synthesize and characterize 300,000+ proteins over four years, generating ~30 million data points — one of the largest publicly available datasets for AI-guided protein engineering. Data and AI models will be released open-access. Cell-free protein synthesis technology pioneered at Northwestern enables the design-build-test-learn cycle. Led by Julius Lucks (PI), with co-PIs Danielle Tullman-Ercek, Han Liu, and Michael Jewett (Stanford). Positioned as the protein-engineering hub within the national PCL network, connected to other AI "self-driving" labs. Part of the U.S. government's Genesis Mission to harness AI for scientific discovery. https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2026/07/ai-directed-protein-engineering-cloud-lab-receives-20-million-from-nsf

### Nature Reviews Drug Discovery: designing small-molecule binding proteins (Aug 4 2026)

Nature Reviews Drug Discovery highlighted the NISE method (Nature, Jun 2026) for zero-shot design of drug-binding proteins. The de novo design of proteins that recognize specific small molecules is complicated by simultaneous optimization of protein sequence, structure, and ligand conformation. Applications include drug delivery carriers, protective shells, and biosensors. The NISE paper achieved 100% success on exatecan, 83% on apixaban, with Kd = 80 pM. This represents a new capability: designing proteins that bind drug molecules from scratch, with implications for drug delivery and biosensing. https://www.nature.com/articles/d41573-026-00129-y
