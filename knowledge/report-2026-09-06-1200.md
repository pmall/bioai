# Report — 2026-09-06

## Findings

- **2026-09-01** — Towards AI publishes detailed statistical reanalysis of Anthropic's Claude protein design campaign. Beta-binomial fit to the eight published per-target counts reveals the headline 26.8% pooled hit rate masks enormous between-target variance: per-target rates ranged from 80% (TREM2, 72/90) to 0% (MBP, 0/90). Fitted median hit rate for a new target is 18.6%, not 26.8%. 90% predictive interval for a new target: 0.1%–84.7%. Chance a 30-design batch returns zero binders: 20.1% (vs 0.0086% under a flat-rate assumption). Designs needed for 95% odds of at least one binder: 638 (vs 10 assumed under flat rate). The analysis argues this combination — high average, enormous variance, uncalibrated self-assessment — is the shape of agentic AI benchmark results. Signals that headline hit rates from AI protein design campaigns are portfolio statistics, not per-target expectations. https://towardsai.com/p/machine-learning/claudes-protein-design-hit-rate-was-26-8-one-target-returned-0-for-90

- **2026-09-01** — FDA and Duke-Margolis convene Innovation in Quantitative Medicine Summit to establish the Quantitative Medicine Innovation Network (QMIN). Cross-sector collaborative platform connecting FDA, academia, industry, CROs, and patient advocates. Follows final FDA adoption of ICH M15 guidance (Jun 2026) for model-informed drug development. FDA already running AI4TOX program with AnimalGAN for virtual-animal digital twins. Signals regulators are building infrastructure for digital twins as potential control arms in clinical trials — the question shifting from "whether" to "how much responsibility" simulations can carry. https://healthpolicy.duke.edu/events/innovation-quantitative-medicine-summit / https://pharmadevicenews.com/would-you-trust-a-drug-approved-using-virtual-patients-the-fda-is-building-the-framework-that-could-make-it-possible/

- **2026-09-05** — Google DeepMind expands AlphaFold Protein Structure Database to over 200 million predicted structures (262.7M total including isoforms/complexes), covering nearly every sequenced organism. 3M+ users across 190 countries. Database initially launched in 2021 with 350K structures. Signals AlphaFold's transition from breakthrough to mature infrastructure — structural biology's reference dataset is now computational, not experimental. https://time.news/google-deepmind-expands-alphafold-database-to-200-million-protein-structures/

- **2026-09-03** — SimpleDesign published in TMLR: single-stage end-to-end model for joint protein sequence and structure codesign. Mixture-of-Transformer architecture with modality-specific processing plus global self-attention. Trained on 2M+ sequence-structure pairs. Argues multi-stage training (autoencoder → generative model) is unnecessary. Signals simplification of protein co-design architectures. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03377

- **2026-09-01** — BiteNetI published in Communications Biology: deep-learning model locating binding sites of 14 biologically important ion types directly in 3D protein structures. 2–3x higher accuracy than most existing predictors including AlphaFold 3 for calcium, potassium, magnesium, phosphate, sulfate. Seconds per structure. Open-access platform. Signals the "one general model for all binding sites" vision moving closer. https://phys.org/news/2026-09-ai-ion-sites-proteins.html

- **2026-09-04** — Squidly published in eLife: sequence-only tool for catalytic residue prediction using contrastive learning with biology-informed pair scheme. F1 >0.85 across enzyme families, F1 0.64 on low-identity (<30%) sequences. 100x faster than structure-based methods. Ensembled with BLAST. Signals sequence-only methods closing gap with structure-based enzyme annotation. https://elifesciences.org/articles/108186

- **2026-09-04** — AIMe (AI Molecule Explorer) from Boyce Thompson Institute/Cornell: neuro-symbolic AI predicting mass spectra of 100M+ small organic molecules. Generated 800M+ predicted spectra covering all PubChem compounds — 1000x expansion of searchable chemical space vs experimental libraries. Applied to metabolomics, identified novel macrocyclic polyamine found in 57/99 human fecal samples. Signals AI enabling computational chemistry at metabolome scale. https://phys.org/news/2026-09-ai-tool-hidden-universe-small.html

- **2026-09-03** — Helmholtz Munich/TUM publish STV-C8 (Synthetic Transfer Vehicle) in Nature: AI-designed RNA transporters with non-natural geometries outperforming LNPs. Bottom-up construction from protein building blocks + generative AI scaffold. CRISPR/Cas9 delivery demonstrated in pig muscle. Plans spin-off. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10952-3

Carterra CEO: "AI system generated thousands of novel binders in days. Two independent labs validated in weeks." Leerink Partners (Jul 2026) named Carterra core enabling technology in ~$7B Lab-in-the-Loop market.
