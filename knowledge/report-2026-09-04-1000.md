# Report — 2026-09-04

## Findings

- **2026-09-03** — Superluminal Medicines raises $60M oversubscribed Series B for AI-designed MC4R agonist in rare genetic obesity. Lead program targets MC4R (G protein-coupled receptor controlling appetite/energy expenditure) for Bardet-Biedl syndrome and hypothalamic obesity. Phase 1 expected by end of 2026. Daily oral small molecule vs injectable competitors (Rhythm's Imcivree, Neurocrine's Vykat XR). Claims pathway selectivity advantage — only activates certain signaling pathways, potentially safer for chronic use. BVF Partners led; also RA Capital, NVIDIA, Eli Lilly, Insight Partners, Perceptive Advisors. Total raised $213M. Existing Lilly partnership for cardiometabolic diseases/obesity. Signals AI drug discovery expanding into GPCR space for rare metabolic diseases — GPCRs are notoriously difficult to drug but represent ~35% of approved drug targets. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superluminal-medicines-announces-oversubscribed-60-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-lead-program-in-rare-genetic-forms-of-obesity-into-the-clinic-302868367.html

- **2026-09-03** — CNBC reports US/European biopharma startups raised $12.6B in H1 2026, a five-year high. Capital shifting toward later-stage companies with drugs in testing; fewer checks to early-stage. Family offices increasingly active: Duquesne backed Epicrispr ($90M Series C for epigenetic FSHD), Bezos Expeditions and Gates Frontier backed LifeMine ($188M for transplant immunosuppressant). Signals broad biotech capital recovery but with late-stage preference — early-stage AI drug discovery startups face tighter conditions despite the headline numbers. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/family-offices-health-care-biotech-startups-august.html

- **2026-09-02** — Novartis remibrutinib (BTK inhibitor, marketed as Rhapsido for chronic spontaneous urticaria) positive Phase 3 in relapsing MS (REMODEL-1 and REMODEL-2). Lower annualized relapse rates vs teriflunomide, stronger reductions in inflammatory brain lesions, benefit on disability progression at 3/6 months (pooled analysis). No concerning liver signals — key differentiator vs Roche's fenebrutinib (FDA partial clinical hold for liver injury in 2023) and Sanofi's tolebrutinib (abandoned in primary progressive MS after liver safety issues and Phase 3 failure). Novartis plans global regulatory filing for relapsing MS in 2027. Roche plans filing in 2026. BTK inhibitor class safety now the competitive front. Signals BTK inhibitors becoming the next major MS treatment class, with safety profile as key differentiator. https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/novartis-rhapsido-cuts-ms-progression-in-phase-3-trial-teeing-up-roche-battle
