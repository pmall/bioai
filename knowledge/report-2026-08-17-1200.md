# Report — 2026-08-17 12:00

## Protein structure prediction

- **ESMFold2 / ESM Atlas** (Biohub, May 27, 2026) — Chan Zuckerberg Biohub released ESMFold2, an open-source protein structure prediction model claiming to surpass AlphaFold3. Generated an atlas of 1.1 billion predicted protein structures and sequence metadata for 6.8 billion proteins, mostly metagenomic. Fully MIT-licensed, no commercial restrictions. Includes validated binder design protocol achieving nanomolar affinities for cancer/immunology targets. Outperforms AF3 on antibody-antigen complex prediction. https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01686-3

## Protein design

- **VibeGen** (MIT, Mar 26, 2026; *Matter*) — AI model that designs proteins based on their dynamics (vibration, flexing, shape-shifting) rather than static structure. Uses diffusion model with two cooperating agents (designer + predictor) to generate de novo sequences with targeted motion profiles. Many different sequences/folds can satisfy the same vibrational target (functional degeneracy). Applications in therapeutics, biomaterials, self-healing materials. https://news.mit.edu/2026/mit-engineers-design-proteins-by-motion-not-just-shape-0326

## Pharma-AI infrastructure

- **Lilly TuneLab** (Eli Lilly, launched ~Sep 2025, expanding 2026) — AI/ML platform giving biotech partners access to drug discovery models trained on >$1B of Lilly proprietary data. Uses NVIDIA BioNeMo + FLARE federated learning (via Rhino FCP). ~16 ready-to-use models across ADMET, discovery, preclinical workflows. Partners include Circle Pharma, insitro, Firefly Bio, Superluminal Medicines. In vivo small molecule predictive models coming exclusively on TuneLab. https://discover-pharma.com/lilly-launches-1-billion-tunelab-ai-platform-to-boost-biotech-drug-discovery/

- **Verseon physics-based drug design** (Jul 22, 2026) — Deep Quantum Modeling platform starts with protein pocket, uses quantum-physics calculations to design novel structures atom by atom. AI enters only after synthesis and testing to propose variations. CEO argues AI is "good at interpolation and terrible at extrapolation." European Patent Office granted new patent for PROAC anticoagulant program (Jul 15, 2026). Seven programs, 14 named candidates, mostly preclinical. https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/verseon-bets-physics-can-take-drug-design-beyond-ais-training-data/

## Key observation

The protein structure prediction landscape has shifted decisively with ESMFold2: an open-source model that surpasses AlphaFold3 on complex prediction, releasing 5x more structures than the AlphaFold database. Combined with VibeGen's dynamics-first design approach and Verseon's physics-first drug design philosophy, the field is bifurcating on two fronts: (1) open vs. proprietary models (Biohub/ESMFold2 vs. Isomorphic Labs), and (2) structure-based vs. dynamics/physics-based design paradigms. Lilly TuneLab represents a new infrastructure layer — big pharma democratizing proprietary AI models to biotechs via federated learning, creating a collaborative ecosystem while protecting IP. The question remains whether these design advances will translate to clinical proof.
