## HUTCHMED-GSK: $1.3B KRAS-EGFR ATTC deal

HUTCHMED licenses HMPL-A830 to GSK — first-in-class antibody-targeted therapy conjugate (KRAS inhibitor + anti-EGFR antibody). $110M upfront, up to $1.295B total milestones + tiered royalties. Preclinical, expected to enter trials H2 2026. Ex-China rights to GSK; HUTCHMED retains Greater China. Addresses KRAS inhibitor resistance in colorectal, pancreatic, and lung cancers. Signals a new ADC-like conjugate category emerging for KRAS — the most commonly mutated oncogene — with Chinese biotech originating the platform innovation.

## Benchling 2026 Biotech AI Report

Survey of ~100 biotech/pharma orgs actively using AI. Top adoption use cases: literature review (76%), protein structure prediction (71%), scientific reporting (66%), target identification (58%). 50% report faster time-to-target today; 56% expect cost reductions within 2 years. Generative de novo drug design at only 42% adoption — data quality is the binding constraint. 52% allocate 11%+ of R&D tech budget to AI. Signals that AI in biotech has crossed from pilots to operational use in bounded, data-clean workflows, but remains limited in complex, judgment-intensive areas by fragmented data infrastructure.
https://www.benchling.com/biotech-ai-report-2026
