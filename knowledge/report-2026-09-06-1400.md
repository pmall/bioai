# Report — 2026-09-06 (afternoon)

## Findings

- **2026-09-03** — AI BioDesign launches: Allen Institute + UW + Fred Hutch $95M open-science initiative for design-build-measure-learn protein engineering loop. Fund for Science and Technology providing $94.6M over 5+ years ($46.1M Allen Institute, $43.8M UW, $4.7M Fred Hutch). 62 participants, lab at Dexter Yard, Seattle South Lake Union. First targets: protein binders, genetic switches, protein stabilization/degradation systems. Method: AI models propose designs → scientists build/test at scale → measurements improve next model rounds. Deliverables: open models, datasets, assays, reagents, tools, benchmarks. No clinical candidates planned — pure infrastructure for closing the computation-biology loop. Signals that the "Lab-in-the-Loop" paradigm is receiving major institutional investment as open science, not just proprietary platforms. https://newsroom.uw.edu/news-releases/ai-biodesign-project-aims-to-adapt-natures-design-rules/ / https://www.axios.com/local/seattle/2026/09/03/seattle-ai-biodesign-allen-institute-uw-fred-hutch-proteins

- **2026-09-06** — Norstella AI4 2026 presentation: "The AI Productivity Paradox." Kris Kaneta (Chief Product & Innovation Officer) argues pharma's biggest AI problem is no longer model access but converting models into trusted decision-support systems. Norstella Atlas is an agentic AI platform integrating Citeline, Evaluate, MMIT, Panalgo data for pharma-specific workflows. First persona: Atlas CI for competitive intelligence. McKinsey Jan 2025 survey: every pharma/medtech leader experimented with genAI, only minority scaled it, 5% said it became a competitive differentiator. Signals the industry is hitting the "last mile" problem — impressive AI demos that fail to integrate into billion-dollar decision workflows. https://cij.world/pharmas-ai-spending-is-rising-but-the-real-breakthrough-may-be-better-decisions/ / https://norstella.com/find-norstella-at-ai4/

- **2026-08-05** — Biolojic Design publishes multibody platform (bioRxiv): AI-designed "two-in-one" IgG antibodies binding two targets with a single Fv binding surface. 7/7 success rate across diverse campaigns (GPCRs, small cytokines). 9 months from idea to IND-ready candidate. Two multibodies in IND-enabling studies, first-in-human expected 2026. BD200 (TROP2/Nectin-4 multibody drug conjugate) showed deep anti-tumor responses including in models refractory to approved ADCs. Superior internalization, T-cell engagement, and immune suppression vs. monospecific benchmarks. Standard IgG format retains manufacturability and PK. Signals a new antibody therapeutic format where AI enables engineering previously intractable dual-specificity within natural IgG architecture. https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.03.742397v1

- **2026-09-01** — Function-aware masking for antibody language models (MLCB 2026 Oral): Aligns mask placement with functional priors (IMGT annotations, structure predictions) during pretraining. Up to 14% gain on structure tasks, 5.9x improvement on CDR-related tasks. Hybrid strategies balance binding, structural, and biophysical objectives. Parameter-free mechanism for imposing functional inductive biases. Signals that how you corrupt data during pretraining matters as much as the data itself for antibody-specific models. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00518

- **2026-07-22** — AAMFM (Antigen-specific Antibody Multimodal Foundation Model) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University: ESM3 backbone + cross-modal adapter for epitope-level antigen conditioning + Calibrated DPO alignment. 30k preference pairs scored by AF3 structural prior. SOTA on full antibody and CDR-H3 design. One checkpoint serves campaigns against unrelated antigens. Lightweight adapter adds only 5.1M parameters (0.36% of base). Signals that the protein design alignment era (DPO/RLHF for proteins) is extending to antigen-specific antibody engineering. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20057

- **2026-09-04** — Science Advances publishes AI pipeline for intrabody conversion: AlphaFold2 + ProteinMPNN + live-cell screening converts antibody sequences to functional scFv intrabodies. 73% success rate (19/26) vs. 5-10% standard approach. 127 designs tested, 51 functional (41% per-design success). Tripled number of intrabodies targeting histone modifications. Open-source code. Signals AI solving the "last mile" problem of making designed proteins work inside living cells, not just in vitro. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adx8352

- **2026-09-04** — Leeds University: AI identifies ~800 plant proteins as potential emulsifiers from tens of millions of candidates for plant-based meat. Signals AI expanding into food science / alternative protein applications beyond pharma. https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/09/04/ai-identifies-plant-proteins-for-use-as-enzymes/

- **2026-09-06** — GPT 6.0 Astra coverage: OpenAI's latest model positioned for autonomous drug discovery workflows — literature browsing, docking simulation initiation, hypothesis iteration. "Critical" cybersecurity capabilities flagged. Signals general-purpose frontier models increasingly positioning as drug discovery agents, not just research assistants. https://eonsr.com/en/gpt-6-0-astra-and-the-future-of-drug-discovery-and-personalized-medicine/
