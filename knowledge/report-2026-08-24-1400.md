### Insilico Medicine: ISM0900 PCC for cardiovascular risk (Aug 12 2026)

Insilico nominated ISM0900, an oral Lp(a) inhibitor, as PCC for management of cardiovascular risks. Lp(a) is an established cardiovascular risk factor that is independent of LDL cholesterol and has been considered undruggable. This is Insilico's 33+ PCC nomination, expanding beyond fibrosis and oncology into cardiovascular. The company is positioning as a high-volume PCC generator across multiple therapeutic areas.

### DeepCyte DeeTox Atlas: first single-cell metabolomic toxicity reference (Aug 19 2026)

DeepCyte launched DeeTox Atlas, the first single-cell metabolomic reference atlas of drug toxicity mechanisms. ~100 compounds, 300,000 cells, ~500 metabolites per cell, six biological replicates per compound. Foundation model trained to predict toxicity mechanisms for unseen compounds. Enterprise pilots with 3 top-20 pharma companies. Appointed Mona Lakkis (formerly Owkin SVP) as SVP Partnerships. Addresses a critical gap: current AI drug discovery focuses on efficacy but safety/toxicity prediction remains reactive and lab-dependent. The shift from reactive toxicology testing to predictive, mechanism-based AI mirrors the broader pattern of AI moving from design to validation layers.

### BMS becomes fourth pharma to license Chai Discovery antibody models (Aug 20 2026)

BioBucks tracker confirms BMS became the fourth large pharma (after Lilly, Novartis, argenx) to license Chai Discovery's antibody-design models non-exclusively. Structural observation: Chai has now licensed the same models to four large pharmas non-exclusively. ### Recursion-Genentech: First validated neuroscience target from AI map (Aug 5 2026)

Recursion and Genentech advanced the first validated neuroscience target from their collaboration into a joint small molecule early discovery program. The target was discovered through an AI-driven, neuroscience-specific map built from over 1 trillion iPSC-derived neuronal cells, genome-wide CRISPR knockout of 17,000+ genes, and 30 million neuronal cellular images. Genentech exercised the Validated Target Option after pathway, functional, and disease validation. First concrete proof that an AI platform can discover novel biology and advance it into drug discovery. The collaboration is part of a broader $12B partnership with Roche/Genentech.
