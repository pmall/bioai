- **2026-08-27** — Chai Discovery raises $130M Series B at ~$1.3B valuation (reported Aug 27). Total raised now >$225M. Led by General Catalyst and Oak HC/FT. Existing investors Thrive Capital, OpenAI, Dimension, Menlo Ventures, Lachy Groom, Yosemite, Neo, SV Angel participated. New investors Emerson Collective and Glade Brook joined. Key advance: Chai-2 has moved from minibinders (antibody fragments) to full-length monoclonal antibodies while maintaining hit rates. CEO Josh Meier: "what looked like five-year problems just months ago are now getting solved in weeks." https://pharmaphorum.com/news/chai-raises-130m-ai-drug-discovery-platform

Not AI drug discovery per se, but signals continued strong VC appetite for nucleic acid therapeutics. https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26

- **2026-08-26** — DaltonTx launches Dalton platform (Jun 4, reported Aug 26). Founded by ex-AZ/Exscientia scientists. Full drug discovery platform: raw data → model training → molecular design → synthesis → decision-making. Key differentiator: data privacy (project data/models kept separate, not used to train other models). First clients: Sygnature Discovery (CRO) and Bonito Biosciences (CNS). CEO Garry Pairaudeau (ex-Exscientia CTO): "organisational memory" — capturing what worked, what failed, and why. https://pharmaphorum.com/news/meet-dalton-new-ai-powered-drug-discovery-platform
