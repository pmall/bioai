### Matwings: closed-loop protein AI reaches clinical clearance (Aug 20 2026)

Shanghai Matwings Technology announced that GenSci148 Injection, an ophthalmic biologic for nAMD, DME, and RVO, received clinical trial clearance in China. Matwings supported the program through four iterative AI–wet lab cycles covering 222 protein variants. Unlike one-time mutation prediction, the teams repeatedly used experimental results to redesign both molecules and optimization objectives — progressing from biological activity to balancing potency, stability, expression, and formulation viscosity. Results: VEGF-A binding affinity up to 10-fold improvement, thermal stability +4.5°C, expression +27.6%, formulation viscosity reduced by 13 cP. In vivo inhibitory activity observed 84 days after dosing. Matwings' Venus AI stack evolved from Venus 1.0 (used for GenSci148) to Venus 3.0 (VenusREM), integrating sequence, structure, and evolutionary information. Biowings Therapeutics established Mar 2026 for internal therapeutic programs. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/matwings-demonstrates-closed-loop-protein-ai-through-four-aiwet-lab-cycles-and-222-protein-variants-302856288.html

### DeepCyte: first single-cell metabolomic toxicity atlas (Aug 19 2026)

DeepCyte launched DeeTox Atlas, the world's first single-cell metabolomic reference atlas of drug toxicity mechanisms. Built from two independent studies spanning ~100 toxicant compounds, 300,000 cells, and ~500 metabolites per cell. Each compound mapped to a four-level hierarchy of toxicity mechanisms anchored to Adverse Outcome Pathways. Foundation model trained to predict toxicity mechanisms for unseen compounds. Enterprise pilots with three top-20 pharma companies advancing. Appointed Mona Lakkis (formerly Owkin) as SVP Partnerships. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/deepcyte-launches-worlds-first-single-cell-mechanistic-toxicity-atlas-as-enterprise-pharma-pilots-advance-302854632.html
