## Antibody and protein engineering

- **BU antibody-specific language model** (Phys.org, Aug 13; Communications AI & Computing) — Boston University researchers developed CDR-focused antibody language model. 600M parameters, trained on 1.6M naturally paired heavy/light chains. Masking up to half of CDR amino acids during training. 27% improvement in binding affinity prediction across 90K+ engineered antibody variants targeting 6 antigens. Requires far fewer computational resources than larger antibody models. https://phys.org/news/2026-08-ai-biology-antibodies-drug-discovery.html

- **De novo isopeptide bond design** (Baker lab, bioRxiv Aug 15) — Over 50 designs forming autocatalytic intra/intermolecular isopeptide bonds, validated by mass spectrometry and 5 crystal structures. Split-protein designs orthogonal to SpyTag/Catcher. Temperature-regulated crosslinking. Rigid covalent protein rings up to 215 kDa constructed from multiple isopeptide bonds. https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.13.744004v1

- **Non-natural RNA-guided nuclease design** (Nature Structural & Molecular Biology, Aug 12) — ESM-IF1 combined with evolutionary coupling analysis (GREMLIN Potts model) to redesign TnpB, a minimal RNA-guided nuclease. Sequences share only 50-60% identity with natural ISDra2 TnpB while preserving catalytic triad. Masking strategy balances structural input with functional conservation. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41594-026-01867-8

## Protein design models

- **Proteo-R1** (arXiv, 2026) — Dual-expert reasoning framework: MLLM "understanding expert" identifies key functional residues, passes them as hard constraints to AF3-like diffusion "generation expert." Decouples molecular reasoning from geometric generation. Outperforms dyMEAN and MFDesign on multi-CDR antibody redesign. Interpretable, modular interface between reasoning and generation. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02937v1

- **A-CODE** (arXiv, May 2026) — Fully atomic one-stage protein co-design with unified multimodal diffusion over discrete atom types and continuous coordinates. Infers residue identities purely from atom-level predictions. Enables non-canonical amino acid modeling. SOTA on unconditional generation; 10x improvement in one-stage binder design success rate on hard tasks vs. Protpardelle. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03360

- **AgentPLM** (arXiv, Jun 2026) — Agentic protein language model with Reasoning-Augmented Decoding (RAD) that interleaves autoregressive generation with tool calls (ESMFold, FoldX, AutoDock Vina). Trained with Contrastive Agent Policy Optimisation (CAPO). 2.79x improvement in antibody top-10% hit rate. Mechanistic evidence of online error correction without backtracking. https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02386

- **CodeFP** (arXiv, May 2026) — Co-generative protein language model for de novo functional protein design. Simultaneously decodes sequence and structure tokens. Functional-Structural Retrieval grounds function semantics via structural motifs. 7.6% gain in functional F1-Macro and 5.2% improvement in foldability success rate over baselines. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00948

## Closed-loop protein engineering

- **ORI (Ontology Reinforcement Iteration)** (Nature Communications, Mar 2026) — Framework integrating ontology-conditioned decoding with RL from experimental feedback. Closed-loop: generation → experimental measurement → model updating. Lysozyme with 100-fold higher activity than TX-L6 (exceeding T4 lysozyme). Chitinase stable at 85°C. Dual-function enzyme with both lysozyme and chitinase activities, outperforming natural Hevamine A. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41467-026-69855-6

- **GenSLM-generated TrpB enzymes** (Nature Communications, Jan 2026) — GenSLM protein language model generates TrpB homologs that are stable, expressible, catalytically active, and substrate-promiscuous. Enzyme 230 surpasses laboratory-evolved PfTrpB-0B2 on native substrate at both RT and 75°C. Unprecedented substrate promiscuity across all tested non-native substrates. Closest natural homolog (NdTrpB) lacks high-temperature activity and broad scope, confirming creative potential of generative models. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68384-6

- **AI-redesigned starting points enhance enzyme evolution** (Nature, Jul 2026) — ProteinMPNN-redesigned BoNT proteases as starting points for phage-assisted continuous evolution. Redesign confers mutational robustness: redesigned enzymes consistently outperform wild-type in evolution campaigns across 3 substrates. Redesigned protease evolved for ataxin-2 achieved 79-fold greater selected specificity than wild-type-evolved variant. Demonstrates that computational redesign + laboratory evolution together overcome limitations of each approach alone. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10820-0

## Key observation

The AlphaFold team dissolution at DeepMind marks a pivotal moment: the science that won a Nobel Prize is now scattered across Anthropic, Isomorphic Labs, and other ventures. The talent exodus suggests Alphabet's bet is firmly on Isomorphic (drug discovery revenue) rather than DeepMind (open science). Meanwhile, the open-source ecosystem is filling the gap — Boltz-2, ESMFold2, OpenDDE collectively offer comparable or superior capabilities to proprietary alternatives. The most interesting new thread is the rise of "agentic" protein design: AgentPLM, Proteo-R1, and ORI all represent models that reason about design decisions before generating, rather than passively producing sequences. This mirrors the broader AI industry shift from single-pass generation to agentic workflows. The rentosertib Phase III remains the single most important near-term event for the field's credibility.
