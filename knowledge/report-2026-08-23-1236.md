## Findings

### Anthropic acquires Coefficient Bio for ~$400M (Apr 3 2026)

Anthropic acquired Coefficient Bio, a stealth AI biotech startup with fewer than 10 employees, in an all-stock deal valued at ~$400M. Coefficient was founded Sep 2025 by Samuel Stanton, Nathan C. Frey, and CEO Aris Theologis — all from Genentech's Prescient Design computational biology unit. The team joins Anthropic's Health and Life Sciences division led by Eric Kauderer-Abrams. Coefficient was developing AI models for biological research and drug development planning — pursuing what they called "artificial superintelligence for science." This acquisition, combined with the earlier launch of Claude for Life Sciences and Claude for Healthcare, signals Anthropic is building biology-native AI models, not just adapting general-purpose models for science. The deal represents a $400M premium for specialized AI-for-biology talent. https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/03/anthropic-buys-biotech-startup-coefficient-bio-in-400m-deal-reports/

### Alnylam-Inceptive $2B AI-RNA collaboration (Jun 3 2026)

Alnylam Pharmaceuticals partnered with Inceptive Nucleics (led by Jakob Uszkoreit, co-author of "Attention Is All You Need") in a deal valued up to $2B with $30M upfront (cash + equity). Three-year collaboration to apply Inceptive's generative AI foundation models to RNAi therapeutic discovery. Goal: fundamentally reinvent how RNA medicines are designed by learning from Alnylam's 20+ years of proprietary RNAi data. Inceptive's models learn the language of RNA sequences to predict target mRNAs and design novel chemical modifications for potency/efficacy. First deal applying transformer-architecture AI to siRNA design at this scale. https://investors.alnylam.com/press-release?id=29941

### Isomorphic Labs: still no disclosed clinical candidate despite $2.1B raise

Multiple independent analyses confirm Isomorphic Labs has disclosed zero clinical candidates, zero disease targets, and zero program names as of August 2026. The $2.1B Series B (May 12, led by Thrive Capital) was structured as a platform bet, not a clinical milestone announcement. Hassabis said in January 2026 at Davos he expects to enter the clinic "by the end of 2026," but this is a leadership expectation, not a committed milestone. One source (DeFiCryptoNews, Jul 1) claimed Isomorphic advanced to Phase 2 in June 2026, but this appears unsupported by primary sources. The honest framing: Isomorphic leads in capital ($2.1B round, ~$2.6B cumulative), but Insilico Medicine leads in clinical evidence (rentosertib Phase III, ISM6331 Fast Track). Analyst Andrii Buvailo posits that "the AI drug discovery valuation cycle has fully decoupled from clinical proof" — capital is chasing computational promise on its own terms. https://theplanettools.ai/blog/isomorphic-labs-2-1-billion-series-b-ai-drug-design-engine-may-2026

### Insilico rentosertib Phase III initiated (Jul 7 2026)

Insilico initiated Phase III for rentosertib (NCT07687459), 320 patients across 47 centers in China. The study was listed as not yet recruiting with estimated start Aug 30, 2026. Phase IIa showed +98.4 mL FVC improvement (60 mg QD) vs -20.3 mL placebo at 12 weeks. CEO Zhavoronkov: "If we outperform the standard of care and show a disease modifying effect with this drug, it's going to be the first and only of its kind." Rentosertib targets TNIK — an AI-identified target that Insilico sees as implicated in 60+ indications. Phase III primary endpoint is 52-week FVC decline rate. The trial will also gather proteomic data from patients to reveal other potential indications. https://insilico.com/news/xmjsn4l091-insilico-initiates-phase-iii-clinical-tr

### BenevolentAI: continued decline to 69 employees

Revelio Labs workforce data shows BenevolentAI has shrunk to 69 employees as of March 2026, down 81.4% from 369 in 2023. The company had 0 active job postings in 2025 and 2026. Founded 2013, headquartered in London. After multiple rounds of layoffs (2023: 180, 2024: 30% cuts, Dec 2024: further restructuring), the company pivoted from being a full biotech back to "TechBio" — focusing on partnership-driven drug discovery collaborations rather than internal pipeline. Cash runway estimated into 2027. Collaborations with AstraZeneca, Eli Lilly, and Merck KGaA remain. A cautionary tale of an early AI drug discovery company that burned through capital and workforce without clinical proof points. https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/benevolentai/employees
