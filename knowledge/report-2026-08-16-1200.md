# Report — 2026-08-16 12:00

## Antibody design

- **Germinal** (Nature Biotechnology, Jun 23; Nature Methods highlight Aug 6) — Stanford/Arc Institute system for epitope-targeted de novo antibody design. Co-optimizes structure and sequence by integrating AlphaFold-Multimer with IgLM (antibody-specific language model). Tested on four protein targets: 4-22% experimental success rates, only 43-101 designs per antigen tested. Nanomolar binding affinities (best: 140 nM). Robust mammalian cell expression. High sequence and structural novelty. Open-source code and protocols. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03187-0

## Protein engineering methods

- **ProteinGuide** (Nature Biotechnology, Jul 29) — Google DeepMind/Berkeley method for on-the-fly conditioning of pretrained protein generative models on auxiliary information (experimental data, desired properties) without retraining. Works across model classes: masked language models (ESM3), autoregressive models (ProteinMPNN), diffusion/flow-matching models (MultiFlow). Demonstrated in vivo: guided adenine base editor toward improved editing activity. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03207-z

## Structural biology frontiers

- **Conformational ensemble prediction** (Communications Biology, May 8) — Review argues the field is approaching two new frontiers beyond AlphaFold 2: de novo protein binder design (routinely achieving nanomolar affinities) and prediction of full conformational landscapes. New generative approaches approximate Boltzmann-weighted ensembles at a fraction of MD cost. CASP pushing conformational landscape prediction as next frontier. https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-026-10112-3

## Industry context

- **AI drug pipeline scale** — AI-originated programs have grown from ~3 in 2016 to 67 in 2023 to 200+ by early 2026. Over $11B invested across ~348 rounds in 2025 alone. No FDA approval yet, but the pipeline is compounding. The conversion to approvals over the next 24 months will determine whether the field's capital structure is justified.

## Key observation

The antibody design bottleneck is collapsing. Germinal demonstrates that epitope-targeted antibodies can be designed computationally with nanomolar affinities using only dozens of experimental tests — a paradigm shift from thousands. Combined with ProteinGuide's ability to steer any pretrained generative model on desired properties without retraining, the infrastructure for programmable protein design is maturing rapidly. Meanwhile, Insilico's first Fast Track designation and Recursion's first Genentech-validated neuroscience target signal that the clinical and commercial translation layers are advancing in parallel.
