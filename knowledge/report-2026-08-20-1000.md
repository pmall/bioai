## Findings

### VirTues: Foundation model for spatial proteomics (Nature, Aug 5 2026)

VirTues is a general-purpose foundation model for spatial proteomics that learns marker-aware, multi-scale representations of proteins, cells, niches, and tissues directly from multiplex imaging data. From a single pretrained backbone, it supports marker reconstruction, cell segmentation and typing, niche annotation, spatial biomarker discovery, and patient stratification — including zero-shot annotation across heterogeneous panels and datasets. In triple-negative breast cancer, VirTues-derived biomarkers predict anti-PD-L1 chemo-immunotherapy response and stratify disease-free survival in an independent cohort, outperforming current clinical stratification. Open-source: https://github.com/bunnelab/virtues. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10884-y

### Schrödinger BMS Bunsen deployment (Aug 5 2026)

Schrödinger announced a strategic agreement with Bristol Myers Squibb to deploy Bunsen, its agentic AI co-scientist, at scale within BMS's research organization. Bunsen is optimized to execute Schrödinger's validated, physics-based computational platform by planning and running complex molecular discovery workflows and interpreting results — unlike general-purpose agents. The agreement includes developing novel functionality within Bunsen in conjunction with RetroSynth (AI-driven synthesis planning). BMS is described as deploying it at "large scale," making this the most significant commercial validation of agentic AI for computational chemistry to date. https://ir.schrodinger.com/press-releases/news-details/2026/Schrdinger-Announces-Strategic-Collaboration-and-Software-Agreement-with-Bristol-Myers-Squibb-to-Deploy-AI-Co-Scientist-Bunsen-for-Agentic-Drug-Discovery/default.aspx

### Cradle-Lundbeck partnership for CNS antibodies (Jun 3 2026)

Lundbeck partnered with Cradle to deploy AI-powered protein engineering for discovering and optimizing biotherapeutics targeting brain disorders. This is Lundbeck's first end-to-end AI-guided protein engineering workflow, where experimental results are continuously fed back into the model. Initial focus: two antibody programs targeting CNS diseases. Lundbeck estimates AI-driven initiatives already contribute 12,000+ hours saved per week through efficiency gains. Complements a partnership with Denmark's Gefion AI supercomputer. https://www.cradle.bio/blog/lundbeck

### AbCellera: Industry shifting to function-based AI models (Aug 19 2026)

AbCellera's integrated computational + lab platform exemplifies the industry pivot from sequence-based to function-based AI models for antibody discovery. The closed-loop approach — where AI predictions are validated and refined through real-world experimental data — is becoming the dominant paradigm. The company is also advancing its own internal pipeline (ABCL575, ABCL635) alongside partnerships. https://marketchameleon.com/articles/b/2026/8/19/abcellera-biologics-integrated-ai-drug-discovery-function-based-models

### AI drug discovery funding: extreme concentration in H1 2026 (Aug 8 2026)

AI drug discovery fundraising reached ~$2.64B across 12 qualifying announcements between Mar 3 and Jul 21, 2026. Isomorphic Labs ($2.1B) and Chai Discovery ($400M) represent ~95% of capital. Median financing was ~$10M. Half the qualifying announcements were seed or pre-seed rounds representing <2% of total capital. The gap between mega-rounds and typical raises is widening. No qualifying pure-player financing was identified between Jul 22 and Aug 5, 2026. https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/ai-drug-discovery-funding-deals

### AI agents for CRISPR / genomic language models (CSHL, Jun 21 2026)

Jennifer Doudna presented VIPR (Viral Interference Programmable Repeats), an RNA-guided DNA recognition system more ancient than CRISPR, discovered by mining AlphaFold structures with AI. The recognition code was deciphered using a genomic language model. Demonstrates AI agents accelerating fundamental biological discovery, not just drug design. https://centuryofbio.com/p/cshl

### Insilico rentosertib Phase III: the definitive test (Jul 7 2026)

Insilico Medicine initiated Phase III for rentosertib (TNIK inhibitor for IPF). 320 patients, 47 centers China. Phase IIa showed +98.4 mL FVC improvement (60 mg QD) vs -20.3 mL placebo at 12 weeks. The first AI-discovered, AI-designed drug to reach Phase III. Estimated primary completion Oct 2029, NDA Mar 2030. This is the single most important clinical test of the AI drug discovery paradigm. https://insilico.com/news/xmjsn4l091-insilico-initiates-phase-iii-clinical-tr
