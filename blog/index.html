What's on the radar

  • Telesis Bio Gibson SOLA: 36-hour closed-loop DNA synthesis

    On-premises enzymatic DNA synthesis being licensed by multiple AI-native drug discovery companies. Removes external DNA supply chain bottleneck. 100s-1000s of constructs per day for antibody expression.

  • Cytiva CTO: AI extends into bioprocessing and manufacturing

    Generative AI predicting manufacturability — expression, purification, formulation. Tahoe Therapeutics ($30M) building AI cell models. Vision of digital twins for biomanufacturing.

  • Merck-Moderna intismeran: first positive Phase 3 for individualized neoantigen therapy

    INTerpath-001 met RFS and DMFS in 1,137 resected melanoma patients. First time any therapy demonstrated additive benefit over KEYTRUDA alone in adjuvant melanoma. Moderna stock +84%.

  • AI drug discovery funding: 95% in two companies

    H1 2026 ~$2.64B. Isomorphic ($2.1B) + Chai ($400M) = 95%. Median ~$10M. No pure-player financing Jul 22-Aug 5. The "boom" is really an Isomorphic/Chai boom — rest of sector capital-starved.
