What's on the radar

  • Insilico's non-opioid pain molecule

    Insilico Medicine's ISM9528, a brain-penetrant non-opioid pain molecule, outperformed morphine in preclinical models — so surprising they redised the experiments. Novel target, novel molecule, novel mechanism. Clinical trials planned for 2027. If it holds up, this is the kind of breakthrough the field has been waiting for.

  • Recursion-Genentech November readouts

    Recursion's REC-4881 Phase 2 data will be presented at Presidential Plenary at CGA-IGC in November. The first major clinical readout from Recursion's AI-discovered pipeline. A clean result validates the phenomics-to-pipeline model; a messy one reopens the Eroom's Law question.

  • Generate:Biomedicines Phase III

    Generate's GB-0895 (anti-TSLP for asthma) is in Phase III — the most advanced clinical program from a generative AI protein design company. A positive readout would be the first Phase III success from a purely AI-designed therapeutic protein.
