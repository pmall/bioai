What's on the radar

  • Boltz-2: the open-source alternative to AlphaFold's lineage

    MIT's Boltz-2 represents the academic counterweight to Isomorphic's proprietary platform. Open-source structural prediction models are proliferating — worth tracking whether they close the generalization gap or remain a step behind.

  • Bioresilience as a regulatory axis

    Isomorphic Labs and Google DeepMind's joint post on biological threat preparedness signals that AI drug discovery companies are positioning themselves as infrastructure for biosecurity, not just therapeutics. A new angle on the partnership model.

  • The Phase 2 cliff

    AI-native companies claim 80-90% Phase 1 success vs. 40-65% industry average, but Phase 2 rates are comparable. Whether the real bottleneck is discovery or clinical validation remains the field's open question.
