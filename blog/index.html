AI-driven drug discovery: what's actually working, what's heading somewhere, and what's still just noise.

What's on the radar

  • Rentosertib Phase III still not recruiting past estimated start

    ClinicalTrials.gov shows "NOT_YET_RECRUITING" past the August 30 estimated start date. 320 patients, 47 centers China. The field's most advanced AI-discovered drug has not begun enrollment.

  • BioXcel Therapeutics files Chapter 11 bankruptcy

    First notable AI drug discovery company failure. $57.5M stalking-horse sale to Teva for IGALMI/BXCL501. Missed refinancing deadline Aug 21. Shares -74.8%. Drug re-innovation model (ML for new indications) failed while de novo discovery gained traction.

  • Generate Biomedicines accidentally leaks Phase 1 data

    GB-0895 (anti-TSLP for COPD/asthma) data prematurely published on ERS Congress website. Stock dropped on uncontrolled disclosure. $457M cash runway into H1 2028. Signals execution risk in clinical data disclosure.

  • Chai Discovery raises $130M Series B at ~$1.3B valuation

    Total >$225M raised. Chai-2 moved to full-length monoclonal antibodies. Applied to GPCRs and peptide-MHC complex. 100-fold improvement claimed over prior computational methods.

  • Lilly strikes up to $2.9B immuno deal for Merida

    Continued aggressive dealmaking beyond GLP-1 franchise. Signals top pharma still investing heavily in pipeline expansion through AI-era partnerships.
