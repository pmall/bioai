Protein to Pill

AI-driven drug discovery: what's actually working, what's heading somewhere, and what's still just noise.

What's on the radar

  • Insilico Medicine reports first AI drug company profitability

    H1 2026: $106.3M revenue (+287% YoY), $35.5M net profit, 90.3% gross margin. First full-period profitability for any AI drug discovery company. Revenue almost entirely deal-based ($103.1M licensing, $2.7M software). Total deal value now ~$7.3B. Cash $584.8M. The high-volume licensing model reaches commercial proof.

  • Ainnocence: 91.4% success rate across 58 antibody campaigns

    SentinusAI platform showing 91.4% program success across 58 antibody affinity maturation campaigns. 28 programs now available for licensing. Sequence-only design — no structure, no MSA. Signals sequence-only protein design reaching production-scale validation.

  • FDA approves Mounjaro for cardiovascular risk reduction

    First dual GIP/GLP-1 with CV benefit. SURPASS-CVOT: 13,299 patients, ~5 years. Matches Novo's Ozempic CV approval. Signals GLP-1/GIP class validated for cardiovascular benefit, expanding addressable market to ~40M Americans with T2D.

  • FDA approves LISRAYA for dermatomyositis

    First targeted therapy for this disease. Dual TYK2/JAK1 inhibitor, once-daily oral pill. VALOR Phase 3 showed significant improvement in muscle/skin activity, physical function, corticosteroid tapering. Signals targeted small-molecule immunomodulation reaching rare autoimmune diseases.

  • SK Biopharmaceuticals licenses Biohaven's Kv7 platform for $795M

    $400M near-term cash for opakalim — selective Kv7.2/7.3 activator in Phase 2/3 for focal epilepsy. SK assumes $245M Knopp obligations. Kv7 category now two-horse race with Xenon's azetukalner. Signals pre-data licensing at significant upfronts when licensee has commercial infrastructure.
