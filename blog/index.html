Protein to Pill

AI-driven drug discovery: what's actually working, what's heading somewhere, and what's still just noise.

What's on the radar

  • Inductive Bio launches Indy, a medicinal-chemistry agent that reads the curves

    CRO parsing plus dose-response QC plus SAR plus FEP plus slides, with doubled team productivity claimed and 89% vs 39% (GPT) vs 48% (Claude) on 84 real dose-response curves. Live use at Anvia — agentic chemistry with head-to-head numbers, not just a pilot.

  • QuantaMind claims reactive modeling at DFT accuracy and 100k-atom scale

    Transition-state reactive MLFF in Science Advances: tens-of-ns reactive MD on 10k-atom systems, 0.25s per 100k-atom step claimed, full enzyme-cycle framing, plus a pH-sensitive antibody with 62x dissociation shift at pH 6.0 vs 7.4. Peer-reviewed venue, press-release numbers — physics-first counterpoint to pooled-structure empiricism.

  • LG screens 420k compounds in 24h for a hair-loss candidate

    EXAONE Discovery identifies Rhamsydil, a non-steroidal female-pattern hair-loss candidate for Dr. Groot, lab-verified and presented at the World Congress for Hair Research. On-premise expert AI for IP security — consumer-cosmetic commercialization of AI screening.

  • Electra sets terms for a $325M antibody IPO

    21.7M shares at $14–$16, ~$976M fully diluted; pan-SIRP ipsoprubart in registrational Ph2/3 SURPASS for sHLH (Ph1b 100% OS/ORR, n=12), SIRPγ ELA822 in Ph1 since August. Pricing September 17 — a late-stage antibody IPO window test.

  • PDS Biotech closes $11.3M Nant-led PIPE

    Up to $22.3M total, Soon-Shiong to board, $11M milestone on PDS0301 Ph3 protocol; NCI MSS mCRC signal of 80% 24-month survival (n=22). Balance-sheet repair tied to a late-stage push.

  • Essex repurposes 672 antibodies into intracellular-stable intrabodies

    Charge-rule plus Baker-lab redesign in Nature Communications for Alzheimer's/Parkinson's/Huntington's/MND targets, molecules to be released freely. Second intrabody win after the 73% de novo pipeline — this time by repurposing rather than designing from scratch.

  • Graph AI raises $13.3M to automate drug-safety case work

    Pharmacovigilance automation claims under 10 minutes per case vs 3+ hours, with aggregate reporting coming September 2026. Traceability pitch — AI moving into the safety and operations layer, not discovery.

  • AI scours 1.2M natural products for a glioblastoma LXRβ hit

    QSAR plus D-MPNN plus DTI ensemble filtered for LXRβ selectivity, BBB properties, and docking/MM-GBSA; 5,6-epoxyergosterol shows selective glioma cytotoxicity with stable His435/Trp443 binding over 200-ns MD. No animal PK or brain exposure yet — computational hit, not a lead.

  • Second binder-to-CAR paper turns affinity into a tuning knob

    RFdiffusion plus ProteinMPNN plus BindCraft pipeline pushed through T-cell expression, signalling, killing, and in vivo durability; affinity, epitope, stability, and expression as tunable parameters. Extends the Chow 1,758-binder failure-mode map; immunogenicity and solid-tumor microenvironment remain unsolved.

  • FDA stabilizes leadership, names first AI deputy

    Mikhail, Davis and Koplow made permanent, plus first-ever Deputy Commissioner for Technology and AI. Follows Elsa plus agentic rollout — regulatory AI remit now explicit.

  • Unified data foundation as AI prerequisite

    Emerj/CDD × Novartis: siloed assay/chem/bio data plus inconsistent annotation equals unpoolable training data. Metadata standards as merge-gate, foundation-first sequencing. Mirrors Benchling data-quality constraint plus Sanofi context-gap thesis.

  • Revvity to acquire Human Cell Design for beta-cell models

    EndoC-βH5 plus NatLine platform paired with HTRF/AlphaLISA/cAMP/insulin readouts for GLP-1/GPCR metabolic screening. Explicit wet-lab validation for AI-enhanced science framing.

  • ADARx revives IPO with three clinical siRNAs

    Agazisiran, onvuzosiran plus ADX-626, with obesity plus Alzheimer's into clinic 2027. $427M cash after $335M AbbVie options deal. RNAi capital active alongside Moonwalk $70M.

  • MutexaGPT turns plain English into enzyme MD campaigns

    Multi-agent LLM at Vanderbilt: halide methyltransferase 40% hit rate with 4x enrichment, cavity 542→2870 Å3, up to 50x on bulkier substrates; amylase cold-adaptation 3.7x at 0°C. Open at enzyhtp.app.vanderbilt.edu.

  • CLSS maps sequence and structure into one space

    Contrastive joint embedding reproduces ECOD/CATH untrained; fragments place with full proteins. For DB search, engineering, evolution.

  • Codex plus ChatGPT as antibiotic-discovery infrastructure

    UPenn de la Fuente lab: Codex writes bioinformatics pipelines filtering billions of sequences, ChatGPT reasons and ranks; living plus extinct genomes. General-purpose LLMs composed around domain pipeline.

  • Arm CEO: chips are the limiter for AI cancer breakthroughs

    Haas says supply-constrained fabs plus HBM; Generate CTO counters GPUs plus proprietary bio-data plus experimentation all required, with BMS live on NVIDIA. Counterpoint to SK 128×B200 plus Novo+AWS buildouts.

  • Takeda R&D chief to retire after pipeline narrowing and AI pivot

    Andy Plump exits June 2027 after ~12 years, following 4,500 cuts, modality narrowing to small molecule/biologics/ADC, and the Nabla $1B AI antibody bet. Same week Takeda rolled Weave Bio regulatory AI across 14 projects — a continuity test for AI execution.

  • Caspian spins out $50M for disease-modifying diabetes via menin

    BVF-led round with Lilly and Kura for oral menin inhibitor KO-7246 to expand beta-cell number and function in T1D/T2D. IND-enabling now. Diabetes as beta-cell restoration rather than glucose control.

  • Solstice raises $225M for next-gen CTLA-4 from China

    Harbour BioMed porustobart: short half-life, neoadjuvant colon plus Keytruda Phase 2 cleared, enrollment Q4 2026. $105M at signing plus up to $1.1B ex-China. Continues the China-originated antibody supply line.

  • BrainChild raises $116M for CSF-delivered CAR-T in DIPG

    Ex vivo B7-H3 CAR-T BCB-276 to CSF, pivotal ILLUMINATE Phase 2 in 75 newly diagnosed children, data 2028. Counterpoint to the in vivo pivot — ex vivo still fundable in CNS locoregional, rare-pediatric disease.

  • Owkin K Pro reaches third pharma; Weave Bio cuts IND drafting 97%

    Boehringer licenses the agentic AI Scientist after AZ and Sanofi, following a MOSAIC pilot. Same week Takeda licensed Weave Bio across 14 projects. Agentic biology deployment count becomes the rollout metric.

  • Six-paper arc from halicin to 863k-peptide AMPSphere

    AICell digest traces antibiotic AI from halicin through abaucin, explainable GNNs, generative AMPs, gut-AMP mining, to AMPSphere with 79/100 active. Open data and models as infrastructure, complementing phenotypic MOA readers.

  • Merck KGaA buys oral fertility programs from PostEra

    Mid-double-digit €M deal for an oral FSHR agonist for IVF plus an LH-CGR agonist — small molecules replacing injectable hormones, with AI credited for solving selectivity and half-life. First-in-class claim, candidate to clinic early next year. AI as qualitative improvement, not just speed.

  • De novo binders become functional CARs — with defined failure modes

    1,758 minibinders vs BCMA/CD19/CD22 mapped three failure modes: tonic signalling, epitope occlusion, off-target activity. A charge filter plus engineering yielded B5.I0 beating clinical scFv binders at high tumor burden. Binding ≠ function — now with a roadmap.

  • Pasteur AI reads bacterial morphology to call antibiotic mechanism

    Near-perfect MOA calls, sub-inhibitory effect detection, and flagging of unprecedented mechanisms, validated on Klebsiella pneumoniae. Phenotypic triage to complement generative antibiotic design.

  • 108 non-experts design 9,886 molecules in 14 days — without synthesis

    Open Discovery Challenge shows ideation is democratized via ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini plus computational ranking. The CACHE blinded benchmark is the counterpoint: AI still doesn't always beat physics on novel targets.

  • JapanFold: sovereign open-source drug discovery on non-NVIDIA compute

    ai& and Tenstorrent launch JapanFold, running Boltz-2, OpenFold3, OpenDDE, Protenix-v2, BoltzGen, and RFdiffusion 3 inside Japan on Galaxy superclusters with 1:1 GPU parity. Free workbench, API, and agent skill. Extends the sovereign AI pattern beyond proprietary compute into open-source infrastructure.

  • Foundation models only work under conditions

    BioSpace opinion argues irreproducible literature and cause-effect shifts bound what foundation models can do in drug design — rejecting "100 years in 10 years" and pointing to network target ID and clinical patient selection as the fruitful ground. Continues the Bender et al. self-assessment.

  • Moonwalk bets on quality weight loss via adipose-targeted siRNA

    $70M Series B for MW101, an adipose-targeted siRNA for obesity with preclinical fat loss and muscle preservation, no appetite suppression. First-in-human late 2027. Signals the non-incretin quality-weight-loss wave extending into AI-designed RNAi.

  • Rentosertib reverses biological age in IPF patients

    Six proteomic aging clocks from Harvard, Oxford, PKU, and Insilico unanimously show rentosertib reversed biological age in 42 IPF patients — peak effect ~3-4 years at Week 4 (30 mg BID), up to 6 years in one clock. Anti-aging effects partially independent of lung function improvement. Signals AI drug discovery entering longevity therapeutics with clinical-grade biomarker validation.

  • AF3-ReD samples conformational states AlphaFold3 misses

    Repulsive bias in AF3's diffusion model enables sampling of open, closed, and intermediate states — AF3's default settings capture only single conformations. Demonstrated on ATP synthase F1β. Drug design where conformational switching matters can now use AI-predicted state ensembles. Signals the conformational sampling problem becoming tractable.

  • Superluminal Medicines raises $60M for AI-designed MC4R agonist in rare obesity

    Oversubscribed Series B. MC4R (GPCR) agonist for Bardet-Biedl syndrome and hypothalamic obesity. Daily oral small molecule vs injectable competitors. Phase 1 expected end of 2026. Backed by BVF Partners, NVIDIA, Eli Lilly, RA Capital. Signals AI expanding into GPCR space — ~35% of approved drug targets but notoriously difficult.

  • Biopharma H1 2026 funding hits $12.6B five-year high — but capital favors late-stage

    US/European biopharma startups raised $12.6B in H1 2026. Capital shifting toward later-stage companies with drugs in testing; fewer checks to early-stage. Family offices increasingly active: Duquesne backed Epicrispr ($90M), Bezos/Gates backed LifeMine ($188M). Early-stage AI drug discovery startups face tighter conditions despite headline numbers.

  • MIT designs novel antibiotics against drug-resistant gonorrhea and MRSA

    Generative AI designed 36M+ compounds, screened computationally. NG1 effective against drug-resistant gonorrhea in mouse model (novel mechanism: LptA protein). DN1 cleared MRSA skin infection in mice. Built on MIT Antibiotics-AI Project. Signals generative AI moving beyond known chemical libraries into novel molecular space for antimicrobial resistance.

  • LifeMine Therapeutics raises $188M from Bezos + Gates

    Funding for fungal genome mining with AI. Signals family office / billionaire capital flowing into AI-driven natural product discovery — a modality where AI's ability to scan vast genomic databases for biosynthetic gene clusters creates value that traditional screening cannot.

  • Adaptyv raises $40M Series A for AI-run wet labs

    Swiss biotech tripling lab capacity by end 2026, opening London lab Q4. Previously $8M Seed. Signals wet-lab capacity becoming critical bottleneck as AI generates more designs — the loop closes only if someone can test what the models propose.

  • AI-designed enzymes break down forever chemicals

    Deep-learning protein folding algorithms used to design novel enzymes that degrade synthetic PFAS compounds. No naturally occurring enzyme can break carbon-fluorine bonds in PFAS; AI reverse-engineers protein structures for this purpose. EPA views as the only viable solution for contaminated reservoirs. Signals AI protein design expanding into environmental remediation.

  • Function-aware masking improves antibody language models

    MLCB 2026 Oral: Aligns mask placement with functional priors during pretraining. 14% gain on structure tasks, 5.9x on CDR tasks. Parameter-free functional inductive bias mechanism. Signals pretraining corruption strategy matters as much as data for antibody-specific models.

  • AAMFM: antigen-specific antibody design via alignment

    Shanghai Jiao Tong: ESM3 backbone + epitope-level antigen adapter + Calibrated DPO alignment for antigen-specific antibody design. 30k preference pairs scored by AF3 structural prior. SOTA on full antibody and CDR-H3 design. One checkpoint serves any antigen. Signals protein design alignment era (DPO/RLHF) extending to antigen-specific antibody engineering.

Twenty thousand structures nobody would share

15 September 2026

Five pharmas pool 20,167 proprietary structures to fine-tune OpenFold3: over 50% high-accuracy vs ~33% public vs ~40% Boltz-2 — and pooled beats siloed. Private measurements carry a number now; the caveat is the result is a blog post, not a peer-reviewed paper, and the model stays private.

The model is commoditized. Validation is the moat.

14 September 2026

Proteina pitches a million antibody validations a month on the explicit thesis that models commoditize and data does not — while a UCSF preprint shows plain reasoning chatbots designing binders at 25% with no specialized pipeline. Hit rates are portfolio statistics; throughput decides who realizes them.

Generators that know the rules — and when they don't

13 September 2026

Symbolic Neural Generation constrains a small molecule generator with inspectable logic rules, reaching clinical-level affinity from five inhibitors — while a Nature Machine Intelligence perspective demands calibrated uncertainty before LLMs steer self-driving labs. Generation is easy; trustworthy generation needs rules plus error bars.
