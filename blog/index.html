Protein to Pill

AI-driven drug discovery: what's actually working, what's heading somewhere, and what's still just noise.

What's on the radar

  • Aizen Therapeutics oral peptide deal

    Multi-program collaboration for oral peptide therapeutics using DaX foundation model. Non-canonical amino acid space at 10x scale. Several million upfront + $100M milestones per target.

  • Beyang Therapeutics $30M Series A

    Oversubscribed round co-led by Legend Capital. ExCEED platform generates novel scaffolds. BT01001 (eye drop, Phase I complete, FDA + NMPA IND cleared) and BT01002 (second-gen Menin inhibitor).

  • Northeastern: open-source AI fails on drug discovery tasks

    Three cohorts tested GPT Researcher, Agent Laboratory, and others. All failed to reproduce human algorithms. Reports rife with errors. Useful for repetitive tasks, requires constant human oversight.
