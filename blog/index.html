Protein to Pill

AI-driven drug discovery: what's actually working, what's heading somewhere, and what's still just noise.

What's on the radar

  • BioNTech halts personalized mRNA vaccine in colorectal cancer

    Phase 2 of autogene cevumeran stopped for futility as monotherapy without checkpoint inhibitor. Shares fell 8%. Roche combo trial in pancreatic cancer continues. Signals personalized mRNA neoantigen therapy requires combination with checkpoint inhibitors — the immune response alone cannot overcome cold tumor microenvironments.

  • AstraZeneca/Ionis Wainua Phase 3: worse than expected

    CARDIO-TTRansform full data: HR 1.14 vs. standard of care (14% higher CV mortality/events). TTR knockdown ~10% less than Alnylam's Amvuttra. Monotherapy showed 29% improvement but the combo was harmful. Stifel: "hard to see a path toward approval." Oral stabilizers remain preferred first-line.

  • Genentech licenses DualityBio's novel-payload ADC platform

    $45M upfront, >$1B milestones for DUPAC — payloads designed to work in tumors resistant to topoisomerase inhibitor-based ADCs (Enhertu, Trodelvy class). DualityBio's 7th major pharma partnership. Signals ADC resistance is now a distinct therapeutic category, and Chinese biotech platforms supply the novel payload innovation.

  • Roche licenses Hanmi Pharm's UCN2 analog for "quality weight loss"

    $190M upfront, up to $2.3B for HM17321 — a non-incretin UCN2 analog that activates CRF2 receptors in muscle to reduce fat mass and preserve lean body mass. Addresses the muscle loss side effect of GLP-1 therapies. Roche now has three obesity strategies. Signals the obesity market is pivoting from "maximum weight loss" to "quality weight loss."

  • Northeastern study: AI still can't reproduce human drug discovery

    Three cohorts tested open-source AI frameworks on drug discovery tasks. All failed to reproduce human algorithms and produced reports rife with errors. Useful for repetitive tasks and statistical support, but complex cases require constant human oversight. A useful reality check against the optimism of recent benchmarks.

  • DeepCyte DeeTox Atlas: toxicity prediction gets its data infrastructure

    First single-cell metabolomic reference atlas of drug toxicity mechanisms. ~100 compounds, 300K cells, ~500 metabolites per cell. Foundation model predicts toxicity for unseen compounds. Three top-20 pharma pilots. Safety prediction remains the neglected layer of AI drug discovery.
