What's on the radar

  • Merck-Moderna intismeran: first positive Phase 3 for mRNA cancer vaccine

    INTerpath-001 met RFS and DMFS endpoints in 1,137 resected melanoma patients. First mRNA-based cancer therapy to succeed in Phase 3. Moderna stock +84%. Trials in lung, kidney, bladder cancer ongoing.

  • AI drug discovery funding: 95% in two companies

    H1 2026 total ~$2.64B across 12 announcements. Isomorphic ($2.1B) + Chai ($400M) = 95% of capital. Median round ~$10M. The sector-level "boom" masks a reality where most AI drug discovery companies are capital-starved.

  • Protein design biosecurity concern

    Frontiers in Microbiology review warns AI-generated proteins may bypass current homology-based DNA synthesis screening. Calls for structure-based screening, model safeguards, and watermarking.
